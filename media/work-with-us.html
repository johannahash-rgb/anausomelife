The editorial fit

We cover the things families actually spend money on.

Not everything belongs here. The best partnerships are the ones readers can imagine in their own closets, kitchens, cars, weekends and gift closets.

The family wardrobe

Classic, preppy and practical clothing, outerwear, shoes, swim, bags and adaptive pieces for real family life — photographed and worn rather than styled once and forgotten.

The gift cupboard

Host gifts, teacher and team thank-yous, family gifts, monogrammable favorites, useful luxuries and the things that make somebody say, “Where did you find that?”

Weekends away

New England hotels, resorts, attractions, luggage, travel tech and products that make a family getaway easier before anyone even leaves the driveway.

Beautiful + useful home

Organization, bedding, kitchen, cleaning, entertaining and home products that are handsome enough to leave out and useful enough to keep.

Tech that earns its keep

Smart displays, calendars, AI gadgets, headphones, safety/location tools and household tech that solves a real problem instead of becoming another app to manage.

Age-respectful sensory + adaptive

Products for older autistic teens and high-support families where usability, dignity and actual sensory payoff matter more than a “special needs” label.

Ways to work together

One good partnership can do more than ten one-off posts.

  • Seasonal or ongoing product seeding
  • Family wardrobe / capsule partnerships
  • Gift-guide sponsorships and product inclusion
  • Review units and longer-term testing
  • Sponsored editorial + social storytelling
  • Affiliate relationships where reader value is real
  • Travel, attraction and hotel collaborations
  • Product-team usability feedback for high-support older-teen access

What a brand gets

Context, not just exposure. We show where a product lives in family life, who it works for, what makes it worth the money and the practical details that help a reader decide.

Gifted product, affiliate links and paid work are disclosed. Product does not purchase a positive review.
