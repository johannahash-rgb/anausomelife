Travel & destinations

Let people feel the place.

The view matters. So do the beds, the pool, the route from the car, and where everyone will eat. Our stories give those details room together.

We distinguish what we experienced, what the property confirms, and what a future guest should check. One family’s visit is not an accessibility audit.

A sample coverage proposal

Agree the story before the stay.

  • One website feature with original photographs, useful observations, and a link to the property.
  • One Instagram carousel and three Story frames with the hospitality relationship disclosed.
  • A printable picture communication card or short visual plan where it supports the visit.
  • An agreed publication target followed by published links and available post-level results with reporting dates.

A proposal to discuss, not an automatic commitment. Scope, dates, guest numbers, room arrangement, hosting, publication timing and reuse rights are agreed in writing. Coverage remains independent.

Product testing

The age-respect test.

Can an older teen with high support needs use it without the product becoming another demand? Does it fit an ordinary household?

What we pay attention to

  • Steps, language, and the path from interest to use.
  • Texture, sound, movement and predictability, described as observed experience.
  • Cleaning, charging, storage, setup, subscriptions, durability, and the work left for someone else.

We describe use and experience, not therapeutic outcomes. Reviews reflect what we find, including limitations.

The standards

Trust is part of the deliverable.

  • Gifted, hosted, affiliate and paid relationships are disclosed where readers encounter the recommendation.
  • A product, stay or payment does not purchase a positive verdict.
  • We do not make unsupported medical or therapeutic claims.
  • We do not use pity framing, staged distress or age-inappropriate infantilization to make disability content more emotional.
  • Usage rights, paid media, whitelisting and exclusivity are separate from ordinary editorial coverage and must be agreed in advance.
  • Private family moments, identifiable guests and staff are handled with care and appropriate permission.
