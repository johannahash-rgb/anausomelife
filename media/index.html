A sample coverage proposal

A concrete starting point.

  • One website feature with original photographs, useful observations, and a link to the property.
  • One Instagram carousel and three Story frames with the hospitality relationship disclosed.
  • A printable picture communication card or short visual plan where it supports the visit.
  • An agreed publication target followed by published links and available post-level results with reporting dates.

We agree scope, dates, room arrangements, hosting, publication timing and reuse rights in writing before confirming a visit. Coverage remains independent.

What to include in a product pitch

Send the exact product and link, its useful features, relevant sizing or dimensions, your timing, and the proposed sample or loan terms. Include any requested social coverage or image rights.

We agree the fit and testing window before anything ships; shipping details are shared privately. Samples do not guarantee coverage or a positive review. Any gifted or paid relationship is disclosed.

Editorial independence, disclosure & family privacy

The standards

Trust is part of the deliverable.

  • Gifted, hosted, affiliate and paid relationships are disclosed where readers encounter the recommendation.
  • A product, stay or payment does not purchase a positive verdict.
  • We do not make unsupported medical or therapeutic claims.
  • We do not use pity framing, staged distress or age-inappropriate infantilization to make disability content more emotional.
  • Usage rights, paid media, whitelisting and exclusivity are separate from ordinary editorial coverage and must be agreed in advance.
  • Private family moments, identifiable guests and staff are handled with care and appropriate permission.
