Work with us

Context, not just exposure.

The strongest fit is something our readers can imagine in a closet, kitchen, car, weekend or ordinary routine. We are opening a small number of founding relationships with brands, destinations and organizations that genuinely belong in that world.

Editorial + social story

An original story with useful context, real-life photography and supporting social coverage. The product or place has to earn the story.

Hosted travel

Hotels, inns, attractions and destinations told through both atmosphere and the planning details a family wants before booking.

Review + long-term test

Products used long enough to talk about setup, fit, cleaning, durability, subscriptions, repeat use and whether the value survives the novelty.

Founding series partner

A recurring relationship around a natural editorial lane can be more useful than ten disconnected posts.

Affiliate, when useful

Affiliate relationships make sense when the product would be covered anyway and the link makes the reader's next step easier.

Licensed creative

Original stills or video for a brand's own channels can be scoped separately. Usage, term, territory, credit and paid-media rights are agreed before production.

Travel & destinations

Let people feel the place. Then help them picture the visit.

A good travel story keeps the romance and leaves the practical details in: the actual room, the water, the arrival, food, parking, nearby stops, the pace of the day and the question worth asking before the reservation.

What a hosted story can cover

  • The room and sleeping setup
  • Pools, hot tubs, water access and changing logistics where relevant
  • Arrival, parking, elevators, exits and the shape of the property
  • Food, nearby stops and realistic family pacing
  • Original photography and a short video story when agreed
  • Useful accessibility details without pretending one family's needs represent everyone

Product testing

The age-respect test.

For sensory, adaptive, household and tech products, we ask a more useful question than “Is it labeled for teens?” Can an older teen with high support needs actually access it without the product becoming another demand?

Steps + language

How many steps sit between interest and payoff? What reading, sequencing or verbal understanding is assumed?

Sensory payoff

Movement, sound, pressure, texture, visual feedback and predictability — described as observed experience, not a therapeutic claim.

Real household load

Charging, subscriptions, cleaning, setup, storage, durability, caregiver workload and what happens when the product behaves unexpectedly.

The standards

Trust is part of the deliverable.

  • Gifted, hosted, affiliate and paid relationships are disclosed where readers encounter the recommendation.
  • A product, stay or payment does not purchase a positive verdict.
  • We do not make unsupported medical or therapeutic claims.
  • We do not use pity framing, staged distress or age-inappropriate infantilization to make disability content more emotional.
  • Usage rights, paid media, whitelisting and exclusivity are separate from ordinary editorial coverage and must be agreed in advance.
  • Private family moments, identifiable guests and staff are handled with care and appropriate permission.
