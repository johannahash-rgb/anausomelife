From the first hello

A clear plan.
A thoughtful partnership.

Small independent properties, family-friendly hotels and destination teams are welcome to start a conversation.

  1. Find the fit

    Share your property, location, possible dates and the experience you’d like us to consider.

  2. Agree on the details

    Confirm the stay, costs, creative brief, deliverables, timeline, filming permissions and any content usage.

  3. Experience, create & share

    Firsthand coverage follows an actual visit. We check factual details and clearly disclose hosted or paid arrangements.

A note on trust

Honest stories make better partnerships.

We keep editorial judgment independent. A hosted stay or payment does not buy a positive verdict. We do not promise audience numbers, bookings, viral reach, press pickup or accessibility certifications. Private family moments, guests and staff are treated with care; filming and identifiable appearances need the appropriate permission.
