A Thanksgiving question
at the Parker.

Sometimes, the most useful part of a hotel story begins before you arrive.

In November 2019, while planning a Thanksgiving stay at Parker New York, I sent the concierge a question: how would we get from the hotel to Fifth Avenue on parade morning?

Sixth Avenue street closures were the detail to work around. Two places can look wonderfully close on a map, while the route between them still deserves a little planning, especially on a holiday morning in Manhattan.

That is the kind of detail we like to leave in a travel story. The setting matters, and so does the question that makes the day easier to picture. Sometimes a good trip starts with a thoughtful note to the concierge.

The address we knew as Parker New York is now Thompson Central Park New York. For a future Thanksgiving visit, check the current parade route and street closures with the hotel and city.

What to expect from a stay

Thoughtful guests.
A clear creative plan.

A typical hosted-stay collaboration brings the experience, original storytelling and useful planning details together. Here’s how we would shape it with your team.

  1. Before we arrive

    We agree on the dates, stay arrangements, a point of contact, the story brief and what your team will receive. Filming spaces, permissions, costs, content rights and timing are settled in advance.

  2. While we’re there

    We gather original photos and footage, notice the details a family needs and experience the place at a real-life pace. Identifiable guests and staff appear only with the appropriate permission.

  3. After the stay

    We create the agreed editorial feature and short video story, with optional supporting copy or a press-release draft. Factual details are checked, hosted or paid relationships are disclosed, and final delivery follows the agreed schedule.

A note on trust

Honest stories make better partnerships.

Our stories are independent, and hosted or paid relationships are disclosed with the coverage. We treat private moments with care and agree on filming permissions and content rights before creating.
