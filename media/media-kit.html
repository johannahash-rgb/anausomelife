The publication

Useful enough to save. Pretty enough to want to read.

An AUsome Life began as a family Instagram account and is expanding into evergreen editorial: seasonal wardrobes, gift guides, New England weekends, useful home finds, family tech and a distinctive older-teen autism/accessibility lens.

We are not trying to be everything to everyone. The point is trust: a reader should know why something made the cut.

The sweet spot: products with taste, utility and a story that still makes sense after the campaign ends.

Best-fit sponsorship lanes

Where we can create repeat value.

Wardrobe partner

Seasonal family capsules, outerwear, footwear, swim, bags and classic pieces worn repeatedly across real family content.

Gift-guide partner

Holiday, host, teacher, team and family gifting with product in context rather than a collage of logos.

Travel + weekends

Hotels, resorts, attractions, luggage and travel products with unusually practical access details.

Home + useful luxury

Bedding, organization, kitchen, entertaining and household products that make everyday life feel better.

Tech + higher-value review

Smart displays, AI devices, headphones, safety/location products and equipment tested long enough to discuss setup, subscriptions and daily usefulness.

Older-teen access

Age-respectful sensory, adaptive, clothing, participation and tech products evaluated through a high-support teen lens.

Editorial standards

Material relationships are disclosed. A gifted product or paid partnership does not buy a positive opinion. We do not make unsupported medical claims, and we do not force a child or teen into developmentally mismatched content for the sake of a deliverable.

The positioning

Profoundly awesome celebrates the person, not the difficulty. We acknowledge that profound autism is a contested term and use it only for our own family experience or when accurately describing a source. Read the language standard.
