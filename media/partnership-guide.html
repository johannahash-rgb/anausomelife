Partnership guide

We would rather build one good brand relationship than collect a hundred sample boxes.

The fit comes first. We are interested in products and experiences that solve a real problem, reduce friction, create a successful participation moment, or give families better information before they spend money. Our high-support autism perspective is specific; the useful questions often travel well across neurodivergent family life.

Priority categories

What we are actively interested in.

Clothing + footwear

Classic/preppy family brands, outerwear, shoes, swim, bags, adaptive clothing and seasonal wardrobe partnerships.

Gifting

Premium but useful gifts, personalization, drinkware, leather goods, New England makers, host gifts and team/teacher thank-yous.

Travel + experiences

Hotels, resorts, museums, attractions, luggage and products that make family outings easier.

Home + entertaining

Organization, bedding, cleaning, kitchen, tabletop, entertaining and products that make the house work better.

Tech

Smart boards, wall calendars, AI gadgets, headphones, charging, safety/location wearables and useful consumer electronics.

Autism + older-teen access

Age-respectful sensory, adaptive and participation products for families whose teenagers still need substantial support.

Collaboration formats

What can make sense

  • Gifted seeding with editorial consideration
  • Seasonal or annual product partnership
  • Wardrobe/capsule sponsorship
  • Gift-guide sponsorship
  • Paid editorial + social package
  • Review unit / loaner for higher-value products
  • Affiliate program
  • Travel hosting
  • Beta/usability feedback

Send the useful details

Product, retail value, gifted vs. paid, timing, required deliverables, usage rights, exclusivity if any, and whether a higher-value unit is gifted or must be returned.

We prefer clear terms up front. We will not trade an expensive product for vague unlimited usage rights or a guaranteed positive review.

We are particularly open to recurring relationships.

For the right brand, a multi-season relationship is more interesting than a single post: family wardrobes that evolve, gifts that return each year, travel products that keep showing up, and home/tech products we can talk about after the novelty wears off.

Representation clause

We will not use cure claims, pity framing, staged distress, age-inappropriate infantilization or intimate private details to make a campaign more emotional. We also will not present one family's experience as the voice of the whole autism spectrum.
