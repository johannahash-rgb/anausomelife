An AUsome Life Launches New England Lifestyle Website

New England living with the practical details left in: family outings, useful products, classic style and profound autism advocacy.

Download press release (.txt)View media kit

MASSACHUSETTS, October 4, 2026 — An AUsome Life, a Massachusetts-based New England family-life publication, today announced the launch of its website at anausomelife.com. Building on the family’s existing Instagram account, the site brings outings, travel, home, classic style and useful everyday products together with a perspective shaped by raising an autistic teen with very high support needs.

The publication looks at what makes a good day possible: a warm hotel pool, a quieter place to pause, clothes that feel right, food that works for the family and enough information to decide whether an outing is worth the drive. Its New England sensibility makes room for both an elegant coastal visit and an ordinary grocery run.

Readers can explore family field notes, favorite places, practical guides and planning tools. Coverage connects the appealing parts of a destination or product with the details that often determine whether it works in everyday life, including pacing, comfort, cleanup, cost and the option to leave early.

Profound autism advocacy is part of that editorial foundation. An AUsome Life addresses significant support needs plainly while keeping the person’s age, preferences and dignity in view. Its approach to universal design asks how clearer information, easier routines and more usable places and products can help a wider range of people participate.

The site welcomes readers looking for good New England places, practical purchases and a more manageable family day, including parents, caregivers, extended family and people who simply appreciate useful details. Personal experience remains identified as one family’s experience, with reported information and future editorial plans distinguished from completed visits and hands-on reviews.

The publication’s editorial standards call for clear disclosure of gifted, hosted, paid and affiliate relationships. A product or stay does not purchase a positive verdict. Journalists, destinations and brands can find editorial information and contact details in the media kit.

Visit anausomelife.com and follow @anausomelife on Instagram.

About An AUsome Life

An AUsome Life is a Massachusetts-based family-life publication. It covers New England outings and travel, home, classic style, useful products and profound autism advocacy, with a focus on everyday participation and the practical details that help families make their own choices.
