For PR + brand teams

Send enough detail to know whether it belongs.

  • Product / experience and retail value
  • Gifted, loaned or paid
  • Available sizes / variants when relevant
  • Timing, embargo or launch date
  • Required deliverables, if any
  • Usage rights or paid amplification requests
  • Why you think it fits this audience

Longer relationships are welcome.
