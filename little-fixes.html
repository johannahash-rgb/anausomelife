The Little Fixes.

Small ideas that make an ordinary day a little easier.

Some of our favorite solutions are not products, systems or routines. They are the things we try with what is already on the counter. They need to work in real life, where the laundry is waiting and there is not always time for a second attempt.

Here is the first: a bendy straw, a bottle cap and a rather useful little adjustment.

01 · Something useful we triedOctober 9, 2026 · Everyday life

A three-step kitchen-counter note

The straw that stays put.

We picked up a bottle of INTENSE® Lowfat Strawberry Milk at Wegmans. A bendy straw made it easier to sip; the little alteration to the twist cap helped the straw stay where we wanted it.

No new gadget. Just a small adjustment to something we already had.

Take off the cap.

Set the open bottle safely aside. Keep the cap separate from the drink while preparing it.

Make a small opening.

An adult carefully makes a narrow slit in the center. Check closely for cracked plastic, sharp edges or loose fragments. If the cap is damaged, do not use it.

Add the straw.

Thread a bendy straw through the opening, screw the cap back on fully and check that everything sits securely. Serve upright.

Where we found it

Purchased at Wegmans: Upstate Farms INTENSE® Lowfat Strawberry Milk, 16 fl oz. The Wegmans product listing is here if you would like to check it out. Availability varies by store.

It is a lovely little reminder that sometimes the useful thing is not another purchase. It is a tiny change to the thing you have already bought.

Our own family photographs and an independently purchased product. This is not a sponsored recommendation or an endorsement from Wegmans or INTENSE Milk.

The sensible bit

This is a straw holder, not a leakproof lid. The cutting is for adults only, with the cap off the bottle. Check for any loose plastic or sharp edges before use, and skip the idea if the cap splits or the person cannot safely use a straw. Keep the milk refrigerated as directed. For travel in a bag, a purpose-made sealed straw bottle is the better choice.
