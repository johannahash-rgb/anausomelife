Familiar beats fancy.

A photo of the actual cup may be more useful than a beautiful illustration. Keep the person’s usual communication available, including ways to say no, stop, help and finished. These printable picture cards supplement a communication system; they do not recreate a TouchChat layout or constitute the PECS® teaching protocol.

Choose the image, word, size and number of choices with the person and their communication team. See ASHA’s AAC guidance and Pyramid’s explanation of PECS®.
