Beautiful is nice. Useful is better.

We write about New England days out, classic clothes, good food, useful products and the practical details that decide whether something actually works in family life.

Our family includes an older autistic teen with very high support needs. We sometimes use profound autism for that experience. The phrase profoundly awesome describes the person, not a claim that disability is easy.

That perspective changes what we notice: the warm pool, the easier closure, the quiet place to pause, the product that removes a step. It is one family's field notes, not a universal rulebook.

High support needs can be profound. So can personality, preference, humor, adolescence and joy.
