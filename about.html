More real life. A bigger world.

An AUsome Life is our family’s field notebook for the places we go, the things we use, the systems we build and the ordinary moments that turn out to matter.

Our son is autistic with high support needs and communicates without speech. That shapes what we notice — not because autism is the only thing worth writing about, but because access, communication, pacing and dignity change the quality of everyday life.

We care about inclusion that works in practice. That can mean an ADA boarding route, a quieter corner, a familiar meal, a staff person who actually knows the building, a visual plan, a shorter visit, or simply a community where people say hello.

A more inclusive world is not a separate world. It is this one, with more people able to use it.
We are also very serious about ice cream.
We like places with personality, room to wander and a reason to come back.
