Meet us

We like a good day. We also like knowing where the bathroom is.

An AUsome Life is about the life we actually live: New England outings, clothes worth wearing again, hotels that work as home base, food people will eat, useful things that earn their space, and profound autism as one real part of the family story.

No tragedy framing. No inspirational confetti. No pretending logistics do not exist.

A good life is allowed to be practical.

Sometimes the difference between a lovely afternoon and an early exit is not profound. It is parking, a warm pool, the right food, a quieter corner, or having the charger you thought was in the bag. We write those things down.

Our family includes an older autistic teen with very high support needs. We use profound autism for that experience. It tells you something important about support. It does not tell you the whole person.

That perspective changes what we notice: the warm pool, the easier closure, the quiet place to pause, the product that removes a step, the restaurant that makes an ordinary dinner possible. These are one family's field notes, not a universal rulebook.

High support needs can be profound. So can taste, humor, annoyance, adolescence and joy.
