Misty Harbor Resort

Wells, Maine

Not a precious hotel. A useful one: more room, an indoor pool, a hot tub, a short ride to the beach, and enough practical structure to make one night feel like a getaway.

A little room for a Maine weekend.

Misty Harbor was our base for a Wells getaway. The pool and the practical shape of the stay belong to the sort of trip we like: time together, a few good stops, and room to come back and settle in.

The simple good thing

Check in, swim, eat something easy, sleep, swim again.

The appeal is not a dramatic lobby reveal. It is removing friction. Condo-style space and indoor water make the weather less powerful, and Wells gives you plenty of simple food and beach options without needing a complicated itinerary.

Accessibility field guide

What we could verify — and what to ask.

“Accessible” is too vague to plan a real day. These are the concrete details we found. Individual needs differ, and venues change.

Verified / published

  • Misty Harbor lists an indoor heated pool, hot tub and sauna
  • The resort is about three-quarters of a mile from Wells Beach
  • Room/unit layouts vary, so accessibility and sleeping setup should be matched directly

Ask before relying on it

  • Exact beds and whether rooms are separated by a door
  • Pool/hot-tub hours on your dates
  • Specific accessible-room and bathroom features you need

A useful access question

“We’re planning to visit on [date/time]. We need [specific feature/support]. Could you tell us the most direct way to access it, where the closest restroom/break space is, and whether anything scheduled that day will substantially change crowds or the normal route?”
