A place field note · Lincoln, Massachusetts

Codman Community Farms

Animals, a familiar path and the self-serve farm store, without a full-day plan.

Come along

The ten-minute
farm visit is allowed.

Sometimes the best local outing is seeing the animals, walking a familiar path, buying something from the farm store, and going home. Codman belongs in that ordinary, useful category.

Looking for practical details?

A close-to-home farm idea

Start with the animals.

For Codman, we would keep the plan close to the simple things: the farm, fresh air and something to notice together. A twenty-minute visit can be worth leaving home for.

Start with the animals you can see from public paths. The farm’s visitor rules ask people to stay out of barns and greenhouses, not touch the animals, and pay attention to electric fencing. Looking is the activity.

A note for your own visit +

There is no required program or full-property circuit in this plan. The grounds, farm store and picnic are separate choices.

An errand, a picnic or home

The store can stay optional.

The self-serve farm store is useful when eggs or food are actually on the list. It does not have to turn the outing into errands. The visit page lists the farm store separately from the dawn-to-dusk grounds.

A picnic is another possibility. Bring it only if sitting down to eat would make the day better, and leave the space as you found it. The farm asks for leave-no-trace cleanup.

See the farm.
That can be the whole visit.

The short version has its own value: animals, a path and home while there is still time in the day.

The local details

Before you arrive.

1Visitor parkingCodman Road arrival+

Use 58 Codman Road. The farm’s own map marks visitor parking immediately in front of the Main Barn/store, reached from Codman Road. Park only in marked spaces. Official farm map · Visit information · Parking rule

The published map does not identify designated accessible stalls or certify the parking surface.

2Main Barn storeStore and restroom map marker+

The store is inside the Main Barn, beside visitor parking, with self-checkout and 24-hour opening. The farm map places a restroom symbol in the same barn complex. Farm store · Official farm map

The restroom symbol does not establish public hours, wheelchair access or changing equipment; ask before relying on those features.

3Farmyard & animalsStay on the public paths+

From the barn area, follow public paths to view the farmyard and pastures. The farm asks visitors to stay outside barns and greenhouses, avoid touching animals and heed electric-fence warnings. Visiting the farm · Farm map

Animal locations change with farm work and season. No measured step-free circuit is published on the visit page.

4Community gardensAt the Lincoln Road corner+

The community gardens sit at the corner of Lincoln and Codman Roads. Use the grassy paths, and leave the plots and their produce to the gardeners. Community-garden visit rules · Farm map

These grassy paths have no published wheelchair surface or slope specification.

Published sources checked October 4, 2026. Grounds are dawn–dusk; the store’s 24-hour opening does not establish restroom hours.

The details that make the visit possible

Know before you go.

01Grounds & farm-store hours+

The farm’s visit page lists free public visits year-round, dawn to dusk, seven days a week. Its self-serve farm store is listed separately as 24/7/365.

Check the same page for anything affecting your visit before setting off.

02Animals, paths & farm rules+

The visitor rules ask you to remain on public paths, stay outside barns and greenhouses, and view animals without touching them. Electric fencing is part of the working-farm environment.

03Ground conditions & facilities+

The visit page does not provide a detailed access audit. If a particular surface or restroom determines whether the day will work, contact the farm about the exact area you want to see and any event affecting parking.

04Picnic & the farm store+

Picnics are allowed with leave-no-trace cleanup under the farm rules. Bring water when it is warm, and make food an option rather than another required stop.
