Accessibility field notes

What actually changes the visit.

  • Mass DCR lists Great Brook Farm as wheelchair accessible, with accessible picnicking and restrooms.
  • DCR says educational programs can provide accessible tours when visitors call ahead.
  • The Universal Access Program is running an adaptive hiking program here November 14, 2026 with specialized equipment and staff support.
  • Parking is $3 April 1–November 30; DCR lists 30 minutes of free parking for ice-cream-stand patrons.

Accessibility is specific to the person and to current conditions. Confirm anything that would determine whether the visit can work.
