Accessibility field notes

What actually changes the visit.

  • Burlington’s public materials identify Wildwood Park as a town recreation site and include it in family-program information.
  • A detailed official accessibility guide for the park was not found. Public listings mention accessible parking, but verify current conditions if that is essential.
  • Restroom availability appears limited/seasonal in public listings; plan as if a full indoor restroom may not be available unless the Town confirms otherwise.
  • For organized fields/courts, programming can change how quiet the park feels.

Accessibility is specific to the person and to current conditions. Confirm anything that would determine whether the visit can work.
