Accessibility field notes

What actually changes the visit.

  • Mass DCR lists Walden Pond as wheelchair accessible, with an accessible Visitor Center and restrooms.
  • DCR lists beach wheelchairs and a universally designed Thoreau House Site Trail among accessibility supports.
  • Main Beach accessibility improvements are ongoing/current; confirm status before a high-stakes visit.
  • Parking fees are waived for vehicles with qualifying disability plates/placards.

Accessibility is specific to the person and to current conditions. Confirm anything that would determine whether the visit can work.
