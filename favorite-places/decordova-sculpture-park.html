The easiest version of the day

Four beats. Nothing heroic.

Arrive

Get the park map and identify a small loop rather than chasing every sculpture.

Wander

Let the next interesting object choose the route.

Pause

Use an open lawn or café break instead of pushing through the full property.

Leave

One or two memorable sculptures can be a complete visit.

Accessibility field notes

What actually changes the visit.

  • Accessible parking is available near the entrance and in the main lot.
  • The property has at least one accessible pathway, but many areas include grass, uneven ground and slopes; some sculptures are on steeper terrain.
  • Indoor galleries are currently closed for renovation, while the Sculpture Park, café and store remain open.
  • If a specific sculpture or route is the goal, call ahead to confirm the most accessible current path.

Accessibility is specific to the person and to current conditions. Confirm anything that would determine whether the visit can work.
