Accessibility field notes

What actually changes the visit.

  • LaCascia’s does not publish a detailed accessibility page on its website. Call if step-free entry, counter access or another accommodation is essential.
  • It is a multi-counter bakery/deli environment and can become busy; online takeout can reduce waiting.
  • The bakery asks customers to disclose food allergies before ordering.
  • For someone who does better with a known choice, review the menu at home rather than making the pastry case the decision point.

Accessibility is specific to the person and to current conditions. Confirm anything that would determine whether the visit can work.
