Accessibility field notes

What actually changes the visit.

  • Boston lists Boston Common amenities including restrooms, shade structure and spray play.
  • Summer Frog Pond rules matter by age: Boston park regulations state people over age 12 may not wade/bathe/use the Frog Pond water.
  • The 2026 spray-pool season ran daily 11 a.m.–6 p.m. through Labor Day; winter skating is a separate seasonal operation.
  • Because this is a busy downtown public space, crowd level can be as important as physical access. Choose off-peak times when possible.

Accessibility is specific to the person and to current conditions. Confirm anything that would determine whether the visit can work.
