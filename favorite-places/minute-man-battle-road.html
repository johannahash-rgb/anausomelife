Accessibility field notes

What actually changes the visit.

  • Minute Man Visitor Center and North Bridge Visitor Center are wheelchair accessible, with accessible parking and restrooms across the park.
  • Trails mix paved and unpaved surfaces; some places are narrow, uneven, steep or stepped.
  • The Battle Road area around Hartwell Tavern is wheelchair accessible, but the nearly 290-year-old tavern building itself is not.
  • Hartwell Tavern’s parking lot has four ADA-designated van-accessible spaces.

Accessibility is specific to the person and to current conditions. Confirm anything that would determine whether the visit can work.
