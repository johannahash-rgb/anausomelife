Accessibility field notes

What actually changes the visit.

  • Accessible parking and restrooms are listed.
  • Two All Persons Trails include the short Ice Pond Trail and the Farmyard Loop; Drumlin publishes detailed surface/slope information.
  • Adaptive items available for loan include hands-free binoculars, audio players, a cane with a small seat and a push wheelchair.
  • Drumlin publishes a step-by-step photo tour/social story, plus print, large-print, Braille/tactile and audio trail materials.

Accessibility is specific to the person and to current conditions. Confirm anything that would determine whether the visit can work.
