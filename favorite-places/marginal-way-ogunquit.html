Marginal Way: take the short ocean section, not the heroic version

Ogunquit, Maine

The ocean is right there almost immediately. The mistake is deciding the only legitimate visit is the entire path from end to end. Pick an access point, walk until the view has done its job, sit, turn around.

The easiest version of the day

Four beats. Nothing heroic.

Bathroom first

Use facilities in town/Perkins Cove before stepping onto the path.

Walk a section

An out-and-back segment can be the whole experience.

Bench

Sit with the ocean instead of treating the bench as failure.

Turn around

Do not let the distance to the far end decide the day.

Accessibility field notes

What actually changes the visit.

  • The Marginal Way Preservation Fund says strollers and wheelchairs can be accommodated and the path is roughly five feet wide in most areas.
  • A special wheelchair is available through the Ogunquit Fire Department; the Fund says users leave a driver’s license while borrowing it.
  • There are no public restrooms directly on the path, so use facilities before starting.
  • Crowds and narrow passing spaces can change an otherwise accessible paved route; early/shoulder-season visits are easier.

Accessibility is specific to the person and to current conditions. Confirm anything that would determine whether the visit can work.
