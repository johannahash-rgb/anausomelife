Accessibility field notes

What actually changes the visit.

  • Life Time publishes separate live hours for club, indoor leisure pool, lap pool, whirlpool, café and other areas; check the actual amenity rather than club hours alone.
  • Guest hours and membership access rules differ from general club hours.
  • The club does not provide a detailed public accessibility guide on the page reviewed. Contact the club about changing-room support, pool access or a specific accommodation before a first visit.
  • Programming can reduce lane/pool availability, so the live schedule is more useful than a remembered routine.

Accessibility is specific to the person and to current conditions. Confirm anything that would determine whether the visit can work.
