L.L.Bean · a family visit

A pause at the trout pond.

Our Freeport camera roll includes a stop at the trout display and another by the games. Those are two different ways of spending time in a store, neither of them much like working through a shopping list.

For another visit, the pond is a good place to begin. Choose the part that interests your group and leave time to watch. Browsing can wait until the people having the outing are ready.

The flagship has since been reimagined. Our photographs record a family visit; use the current store map for today’s layout.

The details that make the visit possible

Know before you go.

Open just what you need.
Arrival & parking+

Start with 95 Main Street, Freeport. The current campus map shows named lots and marks accessible parking in Coyote and Mallard Duck Parking. These are broad locations, not a guarantee of a space or an assessed route.

Event weekends can affect parking. Check current notices and ask which entrance is most practical for your group.

The pond & the current layout+

The August 2026 flagship map places the pond, stream tank and 1912 Café on Level 1. Older family photographs may not show the current layout.

Restrooms, elevators & changing needs+

The current store map marks restrooms and elevators on Levels 1 and 2, and a wellness room on Level 2. A room name does not tell us what changing equipment is provided.

Ask customer service about the exact restroom or changing setup, elevator operation and the route from your entrance before relying on them.

Food & a place to pause+

The 1912 Café has its own hours. Confirm food choices, seating and any ingredient questions before making it part of the visit.

Discovery Park hosts recreation and events. Check the program if music, crowds or waiting will change what works for your group.

Hours & other campus stores+

The flagship’s 24-hour shopping does not establish opening times for the café, Home Store or events. The Home Store is a separate stop at 12 Nathan Nye Street; call 207-552-7701 for its current arrangements.
