Accessibility field notes

What actually changes the visit.

  • The farm welcomes public visits year-round and asks visitors to remain on paths and outside barns/greenhouses.
  • Codman does not publish a detailed accessibility guide on its visit page. Ground conditions can vary on a working farm; contact the farm if a particular surface or route is essential.
  • Picnics are allowed with leave-no-trace cleanup.
  • Animals should be viewed without touching; electric fencing is part of the working-farm environment.

Accessibility is specific to the person and to current conditions. Confirm anything that would determine whether the visit can work.
