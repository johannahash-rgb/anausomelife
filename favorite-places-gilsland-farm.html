Gilsland Farm Audubon Center

Falmouth, Maine

A gentle nature stop just outside Portland with space, birds, meadow views and the kind of pacing that does not ask you to perform family fun.

The simple good thing

Visitor center, one short trail, picnic if useful, done.

This is the place to put between bigger Maine stops. It gives the nervous system some room and still feels like you went somewhere.

Accessibility field guide

What we could verify — and what to ask.

“Accessible” is too vague to plan a real day. These are the concrete details we found. Individual needs differ, and venues change.

Verified / published

  • Maine Audubon lists accessible restrooms and picnic areas
  • Visitor center hours are posted as 10–5 daily; trails are sunrise to sunset
  • A 2,075-foot All Persons Trail is scheduled to open during October 19–23, 2026, after this guide's October 2 check

Ask before relying on it

  • Whether the new All Persons Trail has opened yet
  • Best current low-slope route before that opening
  • Any scheduled school/program group that may change the atmosphere

A useful access question

“We’re planning to visit on [date/time]. We need [specific feature/support]. Could you tell us the most direct way to access it, where the closest restroom/break space is, and whether anything scheduled that day will substantially change crowds or the normal route?”
