About pictures & privacy

Your edits and selected photos stay in this browser tab. There is no upload, account, tracking or server-generated story. Reloading or closing the page clears your changes. Downloaded or printed copies are yours to look after.

Choose a photo you have permission to use. Real, current pictures of the entrance, activity, break place and exit can be more useful than a general illustration. We do not invent photographs of a venue. Check all details with the place before relying on them.

AAC, gestures, familiar objects or other communication may fit better than a printed story. Keep the person’s usual communication available; “no,” “stop,” “break” and “finished” remain real choices.
