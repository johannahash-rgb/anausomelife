A place field note · Freeport, Maine

Wolfe’s Neck Center

Animals, open space and the Maine coast, with a free visit that stands on its own.

Come along

Farm and coast.
Choose your beginning.

For Wolfe’s Neck, we would choose one part of the farm and let that be the beginning. Animals and time outdoors make a distinctly Maine afternoon without filling every minute.

Looking for practical details?

A coastal-farm idea

The free version is a real outing.

The Center’s explore page lists free nature trails and animal barns, with the campus open dawn to dusk. Paid tours and programs are available, but they do not have to organize the entire day.

Begin with a livestock area you want to see. Add a short part of a trail if it suits your group. The car can remain the reset point while you decide whether lunch or Freeport is next.

The local details

Before you arrive.

1Mapped shore-side parkingSouth of the Little River bridge+

The 2026 trail map marks a public parking and picnic area on the shore side of Burnett Road, south of the Little River bridge. Farm buildings are farther along Burnett Road, across the bridge. 2026 official trail and parking map · Driving directions and contact

This is a published parking option, not a verified closest accessible space. For a barn-side drop-off or wheelchair parking arrangement, contact the Center at 207-865-4469.

2Wishcamper Education BarnAnimals near the Center office+

From Wolfe Neck Road, turn onto Burnett Road and cross the Little River bridge for the farm buildings. The Education Barn is free and open dawn–dusk year-round; the Education Garden is beside Little River Farmhouse at 184 Burnett Road. Directions across the bridge · Animal barn visiting information · Garden and farm exploration

Working-farm conditions can change. Ask about the entry and surface needed before relying on a step-free barn visit.

3Farm Store & caféFood and a mapped toilet location+

The Farm Store/café cluster is at 134 Burnett Road, farther along the road from the Center office. The 2026 map places a showers-and-toilets symbol by the campground office/store area. Store/café address · Current food-service hours · Toilet symbol on official map

The symbol does not establish wheelchair access or day-visitor toilet hours. Café hours currently differ from store hours; check the seasonal page.

4Little River Loop trailheadA shorter published loop+

The official trail map locates the Little River Loop trailhead north of the Center office and Education Barn. The loop is 0.9 mile; the full Farm Loop is 3.2 miles, a different commitment. 2026 trail map and distances · Current farm/trail information

Neither distance establishes wheelchair suitability. No surface, grade or step-free standard is supplied by this map.

The details that make the visit possible

Know before you go.

01Barns, trails & opening+

The explore page lists a free campus open daily dawn to dusk, with animal barns and nature trails. Read current notices and follow the visitor signs at the barns.

02Food, tours & programs+

Paid programs and rentals have their own arrangements. The food page lists café hours Friday–Sunday 8 a.m.–1 p.m. and, from Labor Day, store hours Sunday–Thursday 8 a.m.–6 p.m., Friday–Saturday 8 a.m.–8 p.m. These seasonal hours need a date check. Bring familiar food if that is simpler.

03Arrival & the way back+

The contact and directions page lists 184 Burnett Road, Freeport, and 207-865-4469. Save the correct entrance and chosen parking area from the official visitor map before travelling.

04Restrooms, surfaces & busy times+

Use the specific route and facilities information in the map notes, then ask the Center about the shortest suitable route, current restrooms and any activity affecting noise or crowds.

05A checklist to take along+
  • Check weather
  • Open the farm map
  • Wear shoes for farm paths
  • Locate restroom on arrival
  • Pick one animal/trail goal
  • Bring familiar food if café availability matters
  • Keep the car reset easy
  • Check for paid programs only if they add value
