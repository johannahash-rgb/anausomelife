The small adjustments that earn their place in an ordinary week. No grand systems. Just things we tried and found useful.

From our kitchen · October 2026

A straw, a bottle cap, and one less cup to wash.

We picked up a bottle of INTENSE lowfat strawberry milk at Wegmans. A small opening in the cap lets us use a bendy straw without pouring the milk into another cup. Nothing revolutionary. Just a useful little adjustment.

  1. Remove the cap and make a small opening with an appropriate tool, away from the bottle.
  2. Check the cap for cracks, loose plastic or sharp edges; discard it if damaged.
  3. Insert a straw and put the cap back on the bottle.

This is a personal household tip, not a spillproof lid or manufacturer-approved modification. A punctured cap can leak, and cut plastic can pose a hazard. Use adult supervision, check the cap carefully, and avoid this approach for anyone who might chew or swallow plastic pieces.
