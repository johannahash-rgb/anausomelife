The journal / A thoughtful little gift

Harvard, Massachusetts · From the family album

A little research, a short car ride.

Michael’s surprise Shaker history day: a thoughtful gift close to home, with James along for the adventure.

Published October 22, 2026

A little research, a short car ride, and a day planned around something I love. Such a thoughtful gift. ❤️

Michael surprised me with a Shaker history day in Harvard, Massachusetts, with James along for the adventure. I grew up right nearby, and despite being a history buff, I’d somehow never taken the time to explore this part of our local history. Apparently, I needed someone to turn “we should do that someday” into an actual plan. 😂

Harvard was home to the first Shaker community in Massachusetts, with roots going back to Mother Ann Lee’s visit in 1781. The remaining buildings and burial ground are reminders of a community that once farmed, worked, and worshipped here. The National Park Service tells more of the village’s story.

And then there’s the furniture. Those simple wooden chairs, beautiful cupboards, and carefully made tables reflect the Shakers’ beliefs about simplicity, usefulness, and craftsmanship. Even the familiar ladder-back chairs have a story beyond their appearance: they were practical objects made with extraordinary care. The Metropolitan Museum of Art explores that connection between belief and design.

I love learning about the everyday details of people’s lives—what they made, how they lived, and what mattered to them. Getting to explore those stories so close to where I grew up, with my own family, made the day especially meaningful.

It cost very little. The gift was the thought, the research, and having someone else take care of the planning. When you spend so much time organizing family life and caregiving, that part feels especially good.

Thank you, Michael, for remembering the history-loving part of me and making a day of it. ❤️

A little history close to home

For the background and a map of the historic village, start with the Harvard Historical Society’s Shaker history guide. It connects the surviving buildings, burial ground, and Holy Hill with the larger story of the community.

For your own thoughtful outing gift, choose a subject your person loves, research a few nearby stops, and take care of the plan. Leave room to keep the day short or change course.
