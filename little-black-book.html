06 · Community Hall

Organizations we support

These groups are here because their work touches the practical realities of disability, education, research, services, adulthood, advocacy or family life.

Swim Angelfish

Massachusetts · Connecticut · New Hampshire

Adaptive swim lessons and aquatic therapy, with New England locations including Newton, Reading and Salem, New Hampshire. A program we support for its focus on helping more people participate in the water.

Services are paid. Start with an intake request to discuss the swimmer’s needs, location and availability. Funding options have their own eligibility rules; the 2026 Stew Leonard III / Step Into Swim scholarship applications are closed.
