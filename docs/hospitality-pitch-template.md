Internal drafting reference. Do not publish this document in the website build or send with unresolved placeholders. The media page contains public examples and a sample scope.

Subject: [Property]: [specific story idea] for An AUsome Life

Hi [verified contact],

I edit An AUsome Life, an independent New England family travel and lifestyle publication. I'd love to report on [specific, verified property detail] through a story about [useful reader question or distinctive angle]. Our perspective combines original photography with the practical details families want before booking, including sensory considerations and communication access.

Two examples of our work are [relevant published story and link] and [second relevant story and link]. Our [travel brief](https://anausomelife.com/media/#travel) explains the approach.

For an agreed visit, I would propose one website feature, one Instagram carousel, three Story frames and a printable picture communication card or short visual plan, with publication targeted within 30 days. [Confirm capacity and tailor this scope before sending.] Hospitality would be disclosed and the account would remain independent.

Would you consider a fully hosted [one-night/two-night] visit for two adults and our teenager during [authorized date window]? We need separate sleeping space and would welcome your recommendation for a suitable room configuration and quieter date. Hosting would need to include the room and mandatory fees, confirmed in writing before arrangements are made.

Warmly,

## Before sending

Verify the contact, property detail, actual sender identity, date window, examples, scope and capacity. Include only current, dated audience evidence; omit unsupported metrics. The ~6,000 Instagram figure used in earlier outreach was an approximate owner-provided follower count, not independently verified reach. A current Instagram analytics connection was not available in Metricool during the October 14 review. No website audience claims were verified.

Use the live media page rather than an old generic attachment. Keep the final message concise (roughly 180–250 words), property-specific and free of template instructions. One follow-up after 5–7 business days if appropriate; close the loop politely after a decline.
