## Publication standard going forward

For each new asset, record its source, author/provider, creation date, intended use, permission/license evidence and any attribution or restrictions. Verify commercial use, editing and redistribution separately. Search results and a source URL alone are not a reuse license. Do not assume that “AI-generated,” “royalty-free,” a logo disclaimer or a citation resolves third-party rights.

Use broad original art direction: preppy New England, warm natural materials, clear subjects, coastal navy and pine green. Do not replicate another brand's characters, distinctive marks, trade dress, interface, script, melody or recognizable artwork. Use ordinary names where needed to identify editorial subjects; do not imply sponsorship or authorization. Our own recurring characters and music remain independent creations.

Research sources are evidence for facts, not permission to copy source photos or prose. Summarize in original words, attribute quotations and preserve official links. Keep current venue facts separate from family memories and imaginary scenes. No invented negative anecdotes, fake endorsements, clinical guarantees or promises of accessibility.

Images of identifiable people require a documented basis and appropriate consent for the intended use; protect minors and vulnerable people, and never publish private care moments or identifying documents. Location metadata must not expose private addresses or routines. Do not add a public upload gallery or child accounts without separate review.

Music needs composition and recording rights; verify the generation provider's applicable commercial-use terms. Do not imitate a real singer's voice or reuse a protected melody or lyrics. A soundtrack cannot be cleared by an image-generation receipt.

## Limits and open items

Existing family photographs, legacy illustrations and the separate video preview were not individually re-cleared in this pass. Their ownership/permission records and any soundtrack licenses still need to be retained and checked before new reuse, advertising, licensing or merchandise. The researched Freeport boot image remains labeled interpretive AI artwork; it is not certified as legally cleared. Do not expand it into a brand mascot, paid endorsement or merchandise without further review.

Public live generation remains disabled. It requires a private server, protected credentials, bounded costs, abuse controls, source handling and server-side input/output policy checks that fail closed. The proposed Vercel connection has not been confirmed; connecting hosting does not supply image/search credentials or establish rights in outputs. Moderation cannot guarantee the absence of every legal issue.

If significant monetization, brand licensing, merchandise or a rights complaint arises, seek qualified U.S. counsel for that specific use. No indemnity, legal guarantee or registered DMCA-agent status is asserted by these edits.
