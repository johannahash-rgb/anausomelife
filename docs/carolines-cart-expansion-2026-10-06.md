# Caroline’s Cart directory expansion — October 6, 2026

Added 40 policy-based locations, expanding the directory from 70 to 110. All 70 existing records retain their evidence and source-review dates. No branch was contacted, and no current availability, cart position, quantity or condition is asserted.

Walmart’s official accessibility policy was reviewed on October 6 and says all U.S. stores provide Caroline’s Carts: https://corporate.walmart.com/purpose/making-shopping-more-accessible-for-everyone. Each new branch address and main telephone number was checked against its official Walmart store page. These are retailer-policy listings, not new branch confirmations.

No new photography, artwork, logos, bylines or personal identifiers were added. The existing URL, styles, map, filters and per-store contact/report links are preserved.
