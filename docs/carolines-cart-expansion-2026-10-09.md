# Caroline’s Cart directory — October 9, 2026 evidence and expansion log

## Editorial outcome
Expanded from **120 to 165** New England shopping leads, preserving all 120 previous store records. **45 new policy-supported Walmart stores** were added. All six existing Wegmans locations were updated from the older general chain-report evidence class to **retailer policy** after checking the official Wegmans specialized-services page. They are not new addresses, and are not among the 45 additions.

The public directory remains a **contact-and-check resource**, not a real-time inventory map. **No branch has been contacted**, no carts have been reserved, and no location is described as verified to have a cart available or working at the moment of a visit. Official store pages verify contact information and a physical location; the cart evidence comes from separate retailer-wide policies or older reporting. Records retain `store_contacted: null`, `cart_count: null` and `cart_location: null` without fabricated details.

This replaces reliance on the indirect 2025 university summary for the six existing listings.
- **Target:** [2016 Caroline’s Cart rollout](https://corporate.target.com/news-features/article/2016/02/carolines-cart) reported nationwide plans, excluding some smaller stores. Do not assume a specific store is supplied today without asking.
- **Big Y and selected Target locations:** Earlier local reporting may justify leads but is not current proof. Historical dates are retained.
- **Wanzl:** [manufacturer's Caroline’s Cart page](https://www.wanzl.com/en_US/products/carts/shopping-carts/various-shopping-carts/caroline-s-cart~p7260) for product and safety guidance.

## Caregiver-first use and future verification

The revised telephone script asks whether a cart is available **and working today**, who can locate it, and what size/weight guidance applies. Families should ask about the seat, restraints, brakes, storage location, and the suitability of the cart for their own passenger, and follow the manufacturer's instructions. The directory intentionally does not promise reserved carts, accessible routes, on-site availability, an appropriate fit for every person, or staff assistance unless the store confirms it.

Recommended further research: directly contact branches (with date and staff role recorded); request reader reports without disclosing private health information; recheck store URLs quarterly and old reports more frequently; explore Hannaford, ALDI and Trader Joe’s only when a named branch or unambiguous chain-wide source supports listings. Do not turn a chain name in an old article into fictitious branch confirmations.
