## Copy for An AUsome Life's own account
Watching James at the beach yesterday, I wished Roseanne B. could see him.

She was the special education director I once thought I was at war with. I wanted a one-on-one aide for James. We disagreed. Looking back—and watching him move safely across the sand—left me with a profound sense of gratitude for her experience and the village around him.

I wrote about that conversation, what least restrictive environment actually means, and questions parents can take to an IEP meeting. A one-on-one aide can be essential support. Every child's needs deserve an individual decision.

Roseanne, thank you for being part of our village.
