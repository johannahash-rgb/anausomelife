# An AUsome Life — consolidated website brief

Updated October 3, 2026. This is the working editorial and implementation brief for the website. The live source is the existing GitHub Pages project at anausomelife.com; do not create a competing site or overwrite newer work.

## 1. The experience

A welcoming, immersive New England family field guide. Refined coastal/preppy typography, navy, cream and coral, warm original detail and readable, high-contrast text. The clarity and orientation of a good town center: a visitor should know where they are and how to get to the useful thing. Borrow the usefulness of strong publication search and navigation, with original branding and no copied proprietary designs.

- One central top menu, clear icons **with text labels**, an all-sections menu, visual directory, keyboard search and mobile navigation that does not hide essential sections offscreen.
- Search every public page, including body text, tools, events, brands, family agreements and editorial policies.
- Blog and guides organized by subject, subtopic and useful next action; obvious breadcrumbs, print, related guide and tool links.
- Large controls, keyboard access, screen-reader labels, 200% text resilience, reduced-motion support and no autoplay.

## 2. Images and editorial voice

- Use the family's authorized photos for lived experiences; do not blur them or repeatedly substitute one outing's photo for another.
- The trout-pond outing is **L.L.Bean Freeport**, not an aquarium visit. Preserve the correction in captions, descriptions, alt text and search.
- Add original illustrations where requested; distinguish invented art from photographs and verified venue diagrams. Maintain clear, recognizable objects in communication materials.
- Warm, direct, specific, lightly wry writing. Place, practical details and ordinary pleasures matter. Avoid fabricated visits, quotations, memories or researched claims presented as personal observation.
- Aim for substantial introductory outing narratives (about 400 words where supported), followed by usable field notes; no filler to reach a count.
- Public identity remains the AUsome family / Meet us. Respect privacy, age and dignity; do not publish private care or health details.

## 3. Favorite places and the calendar

- At least 20 substantial place guides, with maps, official links/contact details, photographs or clearly labeled art, checklist, field notes and editable visual visit plan.
- Include the specifically requested places: Rococo; Kittery Food Mart plus Fort McClary; L.L.Bean; Rancatore's; Omar's Bistro once the intended venue and news video are verified. Keep location uncertainties explicit.
- Calendar: state browsing, list/month views, maps, meaningful access detail and a visual card/visit-plan action for each event. Include Lurie Trunk-or-Treat in the watchlist until a current date is verified.
- Research official information and recent visitor reports separately. Record source/date and whether a statement is verified, reported or unknown. Do not claim a venue is accessible based on its name or a generic ADA label.
- Cover step-free approach, wheelchair/adaptive stroller routes, surface/slope/distance, visual and hearing access, sensory environment, seating, exits, bathrooms and adult changing facilities. Mention adult changing as a general access detail, never as a family disclosure.
- Automatic event refresh is unfinished until a successful deployed run is evidenced. An undated watchlist is not a confirmed event.

## 4. Communication tools

- Picture-first card maker: a clear picture on top, familiar word underneath, real-photo option, one card initially, printable sheet, downloadable image, optional illustration workflow.
- Keep written support notes/profiles as a separate tool; do not call a text-only note a picture-card generator.
- Keep all local photos and entered personal details in the user's browser unless a future service explicitly explains a different flow.
- Multimodal guide connects photos, real objects, gesture, sign, speech/vocalization and established AAC; keep usual communication available and preserve refusal, pause and finish.
- Do not call generic picture cards the PECS® teaching protocol or imply compatibility with a specific AAC app without testing.
- AI-generated images should use restrained realistic sketches, be inspected for recognizability, and never invent actual venue access. Automatic in-page generation needs a real secured service; a copied prompt is labeled as a copied prompt.
- Visual visit stories are editable and uncertain where appropriate, not promises or compliance exercises.

## 5. Family roles, home, products and organizations

- Inclusive role-based guidance: paternal notes and comparable practical support across family/caregiver roles. Easy shared activities, small wins, dignity and enjoyment.
- Paternal examples: a simple football activity and a repeatable coffee ritual. Coffee coverage reflects personal taste/experience, not a universal caffeine or health claim. Visual preference: blue-and-white mug, restrained old car-key detail.
- Useful wardrobe and gear guides: familiar shoes, joggers, knitwear, dry bags, tech protection, practical household systems. Check actual products and safe use before specific claims.
- Sortable, linked brands/businesses/organizations directory; distinguish family favorites, supported organizations and commercial relationships. Requested organizations include The Arc of Massachusetts, Profound Autism Alliance, Nashoba Learning Group, LABBB and verified Wolk-related organizations.
- Affiliate/gifted/sponsored disclosures must be real. No invented commission links, endorsements or affiliations.

## 6. Playful stories, music and video

- A preppy New England fun section for people of many ages, with clear one-step participation, original stories and optional original music.
- Original characters and art; no imitation of a host's identity, catchphrases or proprietary character design. Factual brand reporting may use accurate names without implying affiliation.
- Predictable videos: place, one interesting thing, an invitation to point/choose/listen, pause, recap. Captions, readable pacing, clear stop and no flashing.
- Use poster/click-to-watch presentation, not automatically loaded video embeds. Actual YouTube IDs only after a real channel/upload exists.
- YouTube channel branding, channel setup and repeatable outing videos remain a linked production task. Do not claim creation or publication without account and upload evidence; no promises of virality.
