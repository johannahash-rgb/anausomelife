# Picture-card collection — 6 October 2026

## Owner request and scope

The owner requested repair or replacement of the PECS/picture-card generator with a huge beautiful set, then clarified “or both” and “a little more fancy lovely but as user centric.” She approved the new realistic object-picture direction during creation.

Both existing public URLs are retained. The maker and library now use a shared 150-card catalog: six existing reviewed object illustrations, 128 new object/activity/place pictures, and 16 original simple communication symbols. Search, category filtering, editable labels, local personal photos, PNG downloads, arbitrary-size selection and multi-page printing replace the six-item/six-card limitations. No family/article imagery has been repurposed as communication artwork.

- `assets/card-studio/{water,break,apple,toilet,coat,car}.webp`: existing reviewed starter pictures, retained in their established use.
- Public language calls them picture communication cards and original editorial illustrations. Existing disclosure explains generated artwork. The page distinguishes generic place illustrations from actual venue photos and says these are not official PECS materials or its teaching protocol.

## Privacy and preservation

Visitor words/photos stay in the open tab and are not uploaded or persisted. Selecting files accepts JPG, PNG or WebP up to 12 MB and resizes locally for manageable card rendering. No public upload gallery, private personal details, tracking, sign-in or hosting migration was introduced. Printed/exported files are intentionally saved only at the visitor's direction.
