The point is not to conquer the itinerary.

Travel gives us a bigger map, but the rules are the same as at home: protect the things that make the day possible. That might be the familiar breakfast, the closer hotel, the pool everyone actually wants, a taxi instead of another mile on foot, or permission to skip the thing everybody else says is “must-see.”

We write the trip we actually took, including the detours, the recovery time and the choices that preserved enough energy to enjoy the reason we came.

Sometimes universal design is an elevator. Sometimes it’s a pedicab and a good song.
