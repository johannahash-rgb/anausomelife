Using the board together
  • Offer real choices and time to answer. Pointing, eye gaze, gestures, words, the person’s usual AAC or an established yes/no signal can all carry a preference.
  • A picture offers a place to start looking. Check the actual item, price and suitability together before a purchase.
  • Keep “no,” “something else,” “help,” “break” and “all done” available. Choosing to look does not create an obligation to buy.
  • Use the person’s familiar communication system alongside the board. This is an additional option, not a teaching protocol or a replacement for their established supports.
