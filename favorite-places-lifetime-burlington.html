A familiar towel, a bag that knows its job, the promise of dry clothes afterwards. An indoor swim can bring a little summer to a gray New England day, provided the practical arrangements suit the people coming along.

At Life Time Burlington, begin with the particular pool and session you intend to use. General club hours do not tell the whole story. Confirm access, changing and support arrangements, then keep the rest of the visit as simple as you like.

A simple plan for the visit.

Make a picture plan →

  1. Choose the actual session

    Check leisure-pool, lap-pool and Family Swim schedules separately. Confirm your teen can use that pool at that time.

  2. Preview the visit

    Use familiar words, AAC or photographs for arrival, changing, swimming and leaving. Keep a stop or break choice available.

  3. Follow the agreed water plan

    Confirm active supervision and any support arrangements before anyone enters the pool area.

  4. Keep dry things ready

    Put the towel and full dry change where they are easy to reach, with the next step already agreed.

Before the car keys

Getting there.

The club is at 186 Middlesex Turnpike, Burlington, MA 01803. Call 617-761-7100 about the actual entrance, drop-off or parking route you need.

The official club page distinguishes indoor leisure pool, indoor lap pool and Family Swim sessions. Outdoor sections currently show closed; check current notices rather than assuming an outdoor swim will be available.

The details that matter

Access & facilities.

Ask about the specific changing arrangement.

Confirm the private or accessible changing space, entry equipment and support-person arrangements that matter to your visit. If you need a hoist, adult-sized changing table or a pool lift, ask directly about availability, operation and booking.

Keep admission and session rules together.

Membership, guest access, age restrictions and any additional charges may affect the plan. A general pool opening time does not guarantee admission to a Family Swim session.

Quieter periods, adaptive instruction and individualized accommodations are not independently established in this guide. Ask the club about the exact day and session, and retain the answer if it will determine your booking.
