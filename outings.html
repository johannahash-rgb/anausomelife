The lovely part and the useful part.

We love an outing that feels like New England: an orchard lane, a beach off-season, a giant boot in Freeport, an ice-cream stop that saves the afternoon. But the pretty photograph is never the whole story.

Our field notes tell you what we wish somebody had told us before we left the house: where the hard part is, what is easier than expected, what costs more than it should, when to ask a person instead of hunting through a website, and whether a shorter version of the day might be the better version.

A successful outing is the one your family can actually enjoy.
