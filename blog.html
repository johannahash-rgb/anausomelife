We actually went

The places are part of the story.

Not “autism-friendly” as a rubber stamp. We care about the arrival, the noise, the bathroom, the food, the moment that worked, the moment that did not, and whether the place was worth the energy it cost.

Real photographs stay real. Practical access notes sit beside the lovely part instead of replacing it.
