Site information · updated October 3, 2026

Privacy.

Useful tools, with clear choices about your words and pictures.

The current website

An AUsome Life is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. We do not operate visitor accounts, checkout, comments, a public upload gallery or an on-site contact form. We do not currently use our own advertising cookies or behavioral analytics.

GitHub states that GitHub Pages logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. Read GitHub’s explanation. Page and image requests still reach the hosting provider even when a tool processes your input locally.

Picture cards & visit plans

Words typed into the picture-card and visit-plan editors and photos selected from your device are processed in the open browser tab. These tools do not send that text or those photos to us or an image provider, and they do not save your edited plan on our server. Save or print before closing or reloading.

A saved PNG or PDF becomes a file on the device or in the destination you choose. The website cannot delete those copies. On a shared device, close the editing tab and consider where downloaded files and print jobs are kept.

Public event or place links may prefill a plan using information in the link. URLs can appear in browser history and hosting logs, so do not put private names, care information or other sensitive details into a shared URL.

Image prompts & future generation

The image-prompt helper prepares text for you to copy. “Copy prompt & open ChatGPT” places that text on your clipboard and opens ChatGPT; it does not submit your prompt. If you paste it into a separate AI service, that service’s privacy and retention terms apply. Use a general description when a person’s name, diagnosis or private address is unnecessary.

This site does not currently research or generate a new image live for a visitor. Before a live service is introduced, we will explain what is sent, which providers process it, how long it is retained and what choices you have. Your local photos will not be silently sent as references.

External services & contact

Opening a map, retailer, social account or video preview takes you to another service. An activated video player may load content from that provider. Those services have their own privacy practices. Read-aloud uses voices made available by your browser or operating system, which may rely on a separate service.

If you email us, the email providers process the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and address the matter raised. Please leave private care records and unnecessary identifying details out of an initial message.

Questions or privacy concerns? Contact the editor. This page will be updated when the site’s data practices change.
