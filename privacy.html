Privacy.

This site is intentionally simple. We collect as little as we reasonably can.

Current site setup

At launch, An AUsome Life is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. We do not currently operate user accounts, checkout, comments or an on-site contact form.

If you email us, your email provider and ours will process the information you choose to send. External links have their own privacy practices.

Analytics + cookies

We are not currently using our own advertising cookies or behavioral tracking. If we later add analytics, advertising or an email signup tool, we will update this page.
