Las Vegas, Nevada · Full arms & a little freedom

I Don’t Drink. I Don’t Gamble. I Love Las Vegas.

A dawn walk on the Las Vegas Strip, a caregiver’s full arms, and a little love for the people who make room for everyone else’s good day.

October 25, 2026

I’m a New England girl in Las Vegas for a few days of work. It’s dawn. I’m crossing a skybridge, arms full, still on East Coast time, due somewhere soon.

At home, I care for a rare, wild spirit. Much of my life is keeping him safe without making his world small. I’m used to moving through one moment with my mind already in the next.

Then I stop for this.

New York in the desert. A castle beside the palms. Liberty holding her torch above it all. Buildings traveling across the glass.

Here, even the city itself is a traveler.

And a caregiver of sorts, too. Of people, imperfectly. Of a rare, wild spirit of freedom: the part of us that still wants to wander and be surprised. Someone is making a bed, carrying a tray, helping a visitor find their way. I think about all the work that goes into making someone else’s day a little easier, a little more fun.

Full arms here, too.

I recognize that work. I know the pleasure of seeing someone enjoy what you helped make possible. For a few days, I get to be the guest.

A pigeon walks toward the edge of the photograph. A city built to make you look up, and a small bird worth looking down for.

There’s room for this New England girl, too.

My arms are still full. I take the picture and go to work with a little more of the morning to keep.

I can’t wait to go back.

Viva Las Vegas.
