Useful details. ♡

The lovely part and the useful part, on the same page.

There are plenty of sites that can show you a pretty place. There are plenty of sites that can give you a checklist. We wanted the two together.

So an outing here can include the water view and where to sit, the gorgeous hotel and whether the bedroom actually closes, the orchard and the walking reality, the museum and the easiest place to take a break. We are interested in what makes a place worth going to and what makes it possible to enjoy once you arrive.

More real life. More good places. Less pretending the logistics do not exist.
