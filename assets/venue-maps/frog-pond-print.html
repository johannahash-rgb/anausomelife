An AUsome Life / a little map for the bag

Frog Pond, one thing at a time.

Landmarks, not a verified accessible route. Pale paths show geography; short dashed marks show mapped steps. Choose your arrival and return route with staff. Conditions may have changed since the October 3, 2026 source check.

1. Frog Pond

Seasonal skating or spray-pool area. Check the operator for today’s activity and closure notices.

2. Pavilion & café

Service building beside the pond. The City lists winter restrooms; café operation is seasonal. Accessible stall, changing equipment and current opening need confirmation.

3. Tadpole Playground

Playground beside Frog Pond. Play access listed by the City does not verify every item or the route from your arrival point.

4. Carousel

A separate seasonal activity west of the pond. Ask the operator about boarding and transfer arrangements.

Before relying on this: Confirm the step-free entrance, open accessible restroom and any changing equipment. The City lists shaded seating and an accessible portable toilet on the Common; the toilet’s current exact location is not pinned here. No quiet room is verified. Garage vehicle entry is on Charles Street; confirm the working elevator and park exit.
