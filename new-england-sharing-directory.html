First, the official locator

Looking for your Buy Nothing group?

The Buy Nothing Project keeps its official, location-searchable list inside its own app or account. Individual Facebook group links change, so we won't pretend a town has a confirmed group when we cannot check it.

Visit its official Buy Nothing group finder, sign in, and look under Settings → Registered Buy Nothing Groups to search by town or address. Or participate directly in the Buy Nothing app.

A word about this directory

A free item should not require a long drive.

Use the town and state filters below to find a real local link, then check the operator's latest hours and who may participate. Many municipal swap sheds require residency, a car sticker, or a staffed opening.

Listings link to an official municipal page, recognized network, or published organizer guidance—not a guessed social-media group. It's a starting collection, not an exhaustive map of every sharing community.

Keep it neighborly

Take what you can use. Give what someone else can.

Ask before driving, pick up when you said you would, respect accessibility needs and realistic schedules, and leave an item clean and working. Sharing should be a pleasure rather than another household errand.
