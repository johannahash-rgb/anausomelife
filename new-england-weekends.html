The AUsome edit

New England weekends.

The pretty part is easy to find. We care about the part that decides whether the weekend actually works: room layout, pool temperature, food, parking, noise, exits, backup plans and what belongs in the bag.

What we cover

Useful before you book.

Hotels + resorts

Room layout, sleeping setup, indoor pools and hot tubs, parking, food, noise, check-in friction and what happens when plans change.

Day trips

Museums, farms, aquariums, historic sites and family outings with the access details that rarely fit in the brochure.

The weekend bag

Luggage, organizers, chargers, headphones, swim gear, blankets, snacks and the products that make departure less ridiculous.

Water first

Pools, beaches, lakes, hot tubs and other water-centered trips — because sometimes that is the whole point.

Our review lens

Would we go back?

  • Was the room genuinely workable for the family?
  • Could we get food without turning dinner into a project?
  • Was the pool actually warm enough to enjoy?
  • Were there places to pause or leave early?
  • Did the staff handle access requests clearly and respectfully?
  • Was the price worth the amount of family friction it removed?
