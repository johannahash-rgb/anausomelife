A family doughnut stop helped inspire a wider trail.

Glazed & Amused opens its Donuts for Everyone trail with the email and visit notes behind our Congdon's story. The independently written feature explores doughnut shops creating opportunities for people with disabilities and others facing barriers.

It also links to our Wells, Maine field note and free picture choice board, identifying the board as a visitor-made resource rather than a service provided by the bakery.
