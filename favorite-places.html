Water can be the whole plan. So can one scoop, a farm loop, a bookstore, or a room with a pool. These shortcuts take you to places that fit the day you actually have.

Boston, Massachusetts

Frog Pond

The whole Common can feel like a city day. Frog Pond can be just one thing: summer spray, winter skating, or the carousel. Pick the season, do the one thing, then find a bench.

Carlisle, Massachusetts

Great Brook Farm

It has the perfect simple-good-things formula: a working dairy farm, a short walk if you want it, and ice cream. The park is enormous; your visit does not need to be.
