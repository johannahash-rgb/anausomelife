The An AUsome Life field guide

Favorite Places.

Twenty places we love, repeat, or think are worth knowing well — not because every one is perfectly “accessible,” but because the good part is worth finding and the practical part is worth telling you before you go.

Think less “bucket list.” More: Where can we get something good to eat? Where is the water? Where is the bathroom? Can we leave easily? Is the transition actually worth it?

What every story includes

Pretty enough for a magazine. Useful enough for the glove box.

  • The simple good thing — why the place is actually worth it.
  • An “easiest version of the day” visual story.
  • Parking, bathroom, food, sensory/pace and cost notes.
  • What accessibility information we could verify — and what still needs a phone call.
  • Official links, maps and useful tech.
  • A one-page printable accessibility/check-before-you-go guide.

A note about access

We would rather tell you what we do not know.

Access needs are individual, and venues change. Each guide separates published/verified information from questions we would still ask directly. A place appearing here is not a claim that it is universally accessible or sensory-friendly. It means there is something genuinely good there — and we are trying to make the planning part clearer.
