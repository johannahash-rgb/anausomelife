Kittery Food Mart + Pepperrell Cove

Kittery, Maine

One of our favorite kinds of Maine plans: get ordinary, affordable food somewhere local, then take the pretty part for free.

Family-tested route favoriteChecked Oct. 2, 2026

The simple good thing

Grab pizza, a sub or another easy bite at Kittery Food Mart, then drive to Pepperrell Cove for a water-view pause.

This is exactly the kind of place-pairing that makes a travel day easier: the food does not have to be fancy because the view does the work. No reservation, no two-hour restaurant commitment, no need to turn lunch into the main event.

Accessibility field guide

What we could verify — and what to ask.

“Accessible” is too vague to plan a real day. These are the concrete details we found. Individual needs differ, and venues change.

Verified / published

  • Kittery Food Mart's official site lists prepared foods and current contact details
  • The Town of Kittery lists a public restroom at Pepperrell Cove
  • Pepperrell Cove is a protected harbor at Kittery Point

Ask before relying on it

  • Market kitchen closing time if arriving late
  • Any current parking restrictions at the Cove
  • Whether the waterfront area is busy due to an event

A useful access question

“We’re planning to visit on [date/time]. We need [specific feature/support]. Could you tell us the most direct way to access it, where the closest restroom/break space is, and whether anything scheduled that day will substantially change crowds or the normal route?”
