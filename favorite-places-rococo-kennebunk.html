Rococo Ice Cream: a changing flavor board and a little stop worth keeping simple.

The little address book

Rococo
Ice Cream

8 Western Avenue
Kennebunk, Maine

See the scoop shop’s own photographs ↗

A Maine coast afternoon leaves plenty of room for ice cream, especially when choosing a flavor becomes a small adventure of its own. At Rococo in Kennebunk, the possibilities reach beyond the familiar: goat cheese with blackberry and Chambord, strawberry with basil, maple and fig with a little jalapeño heat. These are examples from the maker’s repertoire; let today’s scoop board make the final introduction.

That curiosity has roots in Buenos Aires. Founder Lauren Guptill, a tenth-generation Mainer, spent several years in Argentina before opening Rococo in 2012. The city’s heladerías helped inspire what the business calls “ice cream without borders.” Take a scoop into your coastal afternoon, linger where the day allows, and let something delicious be its own small occasion.

Before the car keys

Getting there.

Use the current scoop-shop address: 8 Western Avenue, Kennebunk, ME 04043. Do not rely on an older Kennebunkport listing. For specific arrival or facility questions, call 207-835-1049.

Choose parking or drop-off before arrival, and allow for seasonal traffic. This guide does not establish a dedicated parking space or a particular route from the street.

The details that matter

Access & facilities.

Check the actual facilities.

Step-free entry, customer restrooms, seating and quiet periods have not been independently verified for this guide. Describe the feature you need when you call.

Read the ingredient information.

Rococo says its products share a kitchen containing common allergens and cannot be guaranteed allergen-free. A vegan option or sorbet should not be treated as automatically allergy-safe. Confirm current ingredients and handling directly with the shop.

The Wells test-kitchen chapter.

Rococo’s Wells test kitchen is part of its adventurous flavor story. The current FAQ still mentions ordering pints for scheduled Wells pickup, but the published test-kitchen hours say “See you in 2022.” Confirm the current location, pickup arrangement and any public tasting access with Rococo before making a special trip.

If you would like to make more of it.

The Dessert Bar at the same Western Avenue address has its own hours and menu of plated desserts, affogatos, floats and drinks. Check that page separately from the scoop shop. For a shorter stop, a simple scoop is still a very good plan.
