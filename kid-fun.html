A predictable rhythm

Every outing video follows the same five beats.

01Where?

A map and place name.

02What?

One interesting thing.

03Do.

Point, count, choose or listen.

04Pause.

Enough time to respond.

05Recap.

Four simple words, then finished.

Designed for real nervous systems

Bright can still be calm.

These videos can be lively without being frantic. We use big readable captions, one idea at a time, no flashing, clear transitions, processing pauses, repeated vocabulary and a recognizable ending. The goal is fun plus orientation — not overstimulation.

For every outing page

Video + photos + map + accessibility field notes + social story + printable checklist. The film is the front door; the practical guide stays right underneath it.
