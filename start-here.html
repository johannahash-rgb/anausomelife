Putting it together

A day out, in three small steps.

  1. Find one place you would enjoy

    A favorite stop, a little fresh air, or a reason to take the long way.

  2. Picture the visit

    Put the arrival, the good part, and the way home into a short plan.

  3. Make room for a choice

    Bring pictures for what to do, something to eat, or time for a break.
