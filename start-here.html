How the site should behave

You should always know where you are.

  • The icon bar stays near the top and uses the same landmarks everywhere.
  • Find anything searches across titles, summaries, places, tools and the words inside guides.
  • Press / or Ctrl/⌘ + K to open search quickly.
  • Long pages still use local breadcrumbs and on-page links so you never lose your place.
  • The full Library remains the deep index when you want filters and sorting.
