Provincetown, Massachusetts · A summer tradition

A summer favorite at Twisted.

A giant pizza slice, glowing windows, and a familiar stop on Commercial Street. Some places become part of your family’s summer.

October 16, 2026 · From the family album

The summer story ↓Before you go ↓

Some places become part of your family’s summer. Twisted in Provincetown is one of ours.

The giant pizza slice out front, the neon glowing in the windows, the evening bustle along Commercial Street—and that smile. This photograph holds so much of what we love about coming back each summer.

There’s something lovely about returning to a familiar favorite. When family outings take planning, having a place already on your “we love it here” list is its own small pleasure. You get to build on happy memories, year after year.

Twisted is woven into our Provincetown summers: a cheerful stop in a town we love, and one of those places that makes a return visit feel familiar before we’ve even walked through the door.

Here’s to summer traditions worth keeping—and getting a photograph along the way.

Before you go

The place: Twisted Pizza, Subs & Ice Cream, 293 Commercial Street, Provincetown, Massachusetts.

Current details: Check the shop’s menu and hours, or call 508-487-6973. The window special in our summer photograph is a memory, not a current price.

Arrival note: Our photograph shows steps outside the storefront. If you need a step-free route, ask the shop about the current entrance arrangements before visiting; we haven’t verified an alternative entrance.
