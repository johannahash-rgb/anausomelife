The field-note standard

Accessibility is more than “ADA accessible.”

Mobility + terrain

Step-free routes, elevators, slope, gravel, grass, mud, snow, walking distance and adaptive-stroller realities.

Sensory + communication

Noise, lighting, crowding, quiet spaces, sensory kits, visual supports, AAC-friendly participation and re-entry.

Vision + hearing

Large print, audio description, tactile or verbal description, captions, ASL and assistive listening when confirmed.

Practical care

Accessible restrooms, changing equipment when published, parking, drop-off, seating, weather exposure and what remains unconfirmed.

We prioritize current official venue information and use public visitor reports as a secondary reality check when helpful. Review anecdotes are labeled as such; they never overwrite current organizer facts. Read our full accessibility field-note method ↗
