# An AUsome Life: standing owner direction

Apply these defaults to new work and revisions unless the owner explicitly asks otherwise.

- Brand outreach default: prefer direct, human routes over creator portals and application forms. Research named PR, communications, partnerships, brand marketing, regional sales, agency, executive or editorial contacts first; use direct email or fax when available. If a brand uses an influencer platform such as Cohley, Skeepers or a similar service, still look for and pitch a relevant brand-side decision-maker directly. Do not require the owner to fill out creator forms unless a brand explicitly replies that its form is the only route it will accept. Keep outreach tailored, concise and specific to a real editorial idea or review need.
- Home/editorial partnership priorities include classic and adaptive/preppy clothing; durable furniture; porch and outdoor living; Adirondack chairs; wind chimes; linens and bedding; pottery; tabletop, glassware and plates; kitchen tools and appliances; sensory-friendly home products such as water play and stepping stones; and useful children/family furnishings where age-respectful and appropriate. Prefer established or mid-sized companies that can reasonably support product seeding without burdening tiny makers. Balance premium heritage pieces with genuine Yankee-value coverage when the lower-cost option does the job well.

- Default aesthetic: the preppiest refined New England, Nantucket meets Vermont, warm natural materials, coastal navy, pine green, restrained cranberry, cream, clear photorealistic subjects. All-ages dignity; no cheap or infantilizing imagery. Preserve recognizable object colors and familiar meaning on communication cards.
- Usability and universal design are central. Lead with one obvious task. Pair icons with plain visible labels. Provide examples next to inputs, mark optional settings, and keep optional adjustments in accessible disclosures and native select controls. Favor good defaults, large tap targets, keyboard access, visible focus, clear headings, readable type, strong contrast, error recovery and small-screen reflow. Never rely on color alone. Do not claim full accessibility compliance without an audit.
- Create and publish only lawful, family-friendly, respectful, non-deceptive material. Do not generate or publish sexual content, graphic violence, hate imagery, exploitation, harassment, abusive imagery, or deceptive real-person images. Respect privacy, consent, intellectual property and the dignity of disabled people. Preserve legitimate, non-graphic everyday health and communication topics.
- Use original or authorized images. Label generated artwork accurately; never substitute it for factual family photos or proof about a venue. Review generated images before publication.
- Never enable a public live image-generation endpoint without enforceable server-side input AND output moderation, abuse controls, safe error handling, and protection of credentials and private data. A client-side keyword check or prompt instruction is only a limited aid, never a security or moderation guarantee.
- The current picture-card tool is local-only for user text/photos. It selects reviewed starter artwork and exports printable cards. There is no live image API or public upload gallery. Do not claim uploaded pictures are automatically moderated or externally generated results are controlled by this site.
- Preserve ongoing repository changes by fetching and checking the latest main branch before publication. This site uses GitHub Pages at anausomelife.com; do not migrate hosting without the owner's direction.
- Keep internal editorial direction internal. Tone notes, brand-voice instructions, prompt-engineering notes, QA/checklist language, implementation notes and owner-only strategy belong in `AGENTS.md` or `docs/`, not public HTML, metadata, search indexes or reader-facing downloads unless the owner explicitly asks to publish them. When public content is generated from internal notes, strip the instructions and publish only the finished reader-facing result.
- The outing studio must support general locations and scenarios, not only a fixed favorite-place list. The intended live flow is internet research first, then original photorealistic imagery and an editable short story. Disambiguate uncertain places, preserve source links, and label generated venue art separately from actual entrance/reference photos. Do not claim live research/generation until the secure service is connected and verified. See docs/outing-studio-live-generation.md.
- Rights and truthful publication are release requirements. A source URL, AI origin, disclaimer or credit is not by itself permission to reuse third-party material. Record the basis for each new asset's intended use; do not restore assets marked not-cleared. Use original ordinary-text editorial cards for directory entries when logo permissions are unverified. Preserve independent branding; no copied characters, scripts, melodies, voice impersonations, branded interfaces or implied sponsorship. Disclose actual paid/gifted/commission relationships next to the relevant recommendation. Keep fictional/proposed visits distinct from documented family experiences. Run tools/check_editorial_rights.py before publication and review its scope limitations; see docs/editorial-rights-review-2026-10-03.md. Do not claim guaranteed legality or complete legal clearance.

- Visual direction refined 3 October 2026: magazine meets Craigslist. Use a compact editorial cover, distinctive real or clearly labeled AI photography, simple original line icons paired with labels, and dense but readable categorized text links. Put useful categories and search near the top. Avoid repeated decorative thumbnails, tall generic hero banners, and walls of equal-weight boxed cards. The maintained homepage body is content/home-entrance.html; shared refinement is assets/editorial.css.

- The owner rejected the remaining flat postcard/clip-art style on inner outing pages. Do not restore it. Run tools/apply_editorial_photography.py after any page generator; it applies the original photographic editorial replacements and current shared style versions. Keep actual family photos and meaningful functional diagrams distinct from decorative images.

- Do not repeat headlines within a page. Keep the editorial headline in its story section; introductions, navigation, interactive checklists and practical disclosures should not echo it. Run `python tools/apply_headline_cleanup.py` after page generators, then refresh the site search index. Preserve existing URLs and section anchors.

- Approved illustration direction (4 October 2026): preserve the refined ink-and-watercolor treatment of the Margaritas jukebox artwork and the Ellis pedicab sketch throughout editorial work. Use natural proportions, fine ink detail, organic transparent edges, warm cream, navy and pine green, with subject-specific color accents. Keep artwork uncropped and without added masks, rounded containers, artificial shadows or tinted filters. Treat real family photographs as documentary images with their original proportions and pixels. Maintain consistent typography, caption scale and spacing, while varying compositions to suit each story. Do not replace approved sketches with generic decorative artwork or apply a blanket photo-to-AI transformation. See `docs/editorial-art-direction.md` and `assets/editorial-art-direction.css`.

- Practical review (5 October 2026): never turn family/article photos or decorative venue art into automatic communication cards. Keep the six clear starter objects and personal-photo editor. Public illustration captions use “Original editorial illustration”; keep generated-art provenance in the rights records and disclosure. Practical guide pages use one visible checklist, not duplicated tab/reminder/visual sequences. Keep communication notes focused on usable print/copy output.
