Preparation should buy freedom.

The goal of a checklist is not to make the family perform a flawless outing. It is to make the outing take less executive function once you are in it.

Our resources cover what we repeatedly need: a quick access call before leaving, a short visual sequence, familiar-food planning, a plan for a quiet break, simple notes that can move between adults, and honest questions for hotels, museums and attractions.

The right information at the right moment can change the whole day.
