A family field note · Wells, Maine

Wells Beach,
with room to breathe.

A September photograph, the Atlantic beyond the railing, and the simplest version of a beach visit.

Come along

The ocean can be
the whole plan.

A beach visit does not need chairs, games, a vast cooler and six hours of commitment. Wells is very good at the simple version: see the water, take a breath and decide what comes next.

Looking for practical details?

From the family camera roll

September, beside the waves.

Our September 2026 photograph keeps the family together in the foreground, with white surf behind the railing. It is a coastal memory without a complicated itinerary attached.

For another Wells visit, we would leave room for that version of the day. Look at the water, walk as much as feels right, and stop before getting back becomes a project. The sea air does not need a full day to itself.

The family photograph records that September visit. The winter drawing above shows another season and is not a picture of our day.

Before settling in

Plan the way back, too.

Wells has a broad beach and a town around it, but parking, sand and the distance back to the car still shape the day. Choose the return point before spreading out.

Dry clothes, water and familiar food are useful things to have ready. Locate the restroom you intend to use before making the beach your base, especially outside the busiest summer weeks.

See the water.
Decide what comes next.

Wind, surf and crowds can change how the shore feels. Keep the person’s usual communication available and leave room to finish sooner.

2Eastern Shore LotParking and seasonal wheelchair information+

The town places this lot at the end of Atlantic Avenue. The town’s beach wheelchair loaner program describes first-come summer use from the lifeguard shack at 513 Atlantic Avenue, ending Labor Day weekend.

October 4, 2026 is outside that stated wheelchair season. Confirm current arrangements with Wells Fire Department at (207) 646-7912. This broad lot pin does not locate the shack or guarantee equipment.

Town beach information
Parking & payment+

The town lists paid parking at the Mile Road lot by the playground and at other beach lots. Its published fee season runs from the Saturday of Memorial Day weekend through Indigenous Peoples’ Day Monday, which includes early October.

Casino Square is listed as free public parking. Check posted limits, closures, payment instructions and space availability on arrival.

Parking locations and current rules
Beach wheelchairs+

The town’s official beach wheelchair program. It describes first-come summer use through Labor Day weekend from the lifeguard shack at 513 Atlantic Avenue.

October 4, 2026 is outside that published window. Confirm present arrangements with Wells Fire Department at (207) 646-7912. Equipment availability, fit and the route you will use need their own check.

Restrooms & changing+

Facilities vary with the beach location and season. No exact restroom, changing facility or step-free beach entrance is established by our map pins.

Ask the town which facility is open for your date and which route connects it with the place you plan to stop.

Food, communication & the return+

Bring water and familiar food, or check the Wells stop you intend to use before arrival. Keep the usual communication system available at the shore.

Bring dry clothes and choose a clear return point. A walk can end when it has done what you came for.
