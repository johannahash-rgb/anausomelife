Las Vegas, Nevada · A night for me

A little self-care, courtesy of Mr. Las Vegas.

Meeting Wayne Newton was a proper bucket-list wish. The music was wonderful. The man, somehow, was lovelier still.

October 16, 2026 · Visited October 12 · Flamingo Las Vegas

The little story ↓Meeting Wayne ↓Access & visit details ↓
In this story
And then I met Wayne.Planning Wayne Newton at the Flamingo: tickets, VIP & access.Little pleasures still count.

Somewhere between the calendar, the work, and remembering what everybody else needs, I keep a little list of things I have always wanted to do. Seeing Wayne Newton in Las Vegas was on it. Meeting him? That was the dream at the very top.

I was in town for work, and it would have been easy to let the evening dissolve into one more email and an early bedtime. Instead, I went to the Flamingo for Wayne: Up Close & Personal on October 12. A little self-care, in a beautifully old-fashioned Vegas sort of way.

Bugsy’s Cabaret is the sort of room that makes a performance feel less like a stadium concert and more like an invitation. There were red curtains, a small band, a guitar, archival pictures, and a lifetime of stories to tell. Wayne didn’t need fireworks. He had something rather more interesting: an extraordinary history and the gift of making it personal.

Music can keep company with you for decades. Certain voices, songs, and the places we associate with them become part of the furniture of a life. I wanted to be there, in the room, to hear the stories behind the songs and see the person whose music had meant so much to me.

The point of the evening was not to escape my life. It was to remember that I have a life of my own within it, too.

For me, self-care is not always a spa appointment or a perfectly quiet hour with a book. Sometimes it is admitting that a wish matters, making the reservation, getting dressed, and going. It can be a big, sparkly thing. It can also be wonderfully simple: a little time that belongs to you.

The part I will keep forever

And then I met Wayne.

The VIP experience meant that after the performance I actually got to meet him, not just applaud him from my seat. There is a souvenir photograph to prove it, although I hardly need the reminder.

I am so delighted I did this. Meeting someone whose music you have loved for years can be a risky business; real life does not always live up to the picture in your head. This time it did. Wayne was every bit as beautiful a person as his music. Warmth like that stays with you.

I left with my bucket-list wish checked off and the rather better feeling of having done something just because it made me happy. I would wish that for every person who spends so much of their time looking after the rest of the world.

The practical details left in

Planning Wayne Newton at the Flamingo: tickets, VIP & access.

Because a lovely night out should not require twenty minutes of frantic searching once you get there, here are the confirmed details and the questions I would ask before booking. The venue information below comes from the ticketing and venue listings; it is not a firsthand audit of every accessible route or service.

The venue
Wayne Newton Theater at Bugsy’s Cabaret, inside Flamingo Las Vegas, 3555 S. Las Vegas Boulevard, Las Vegas, NV 89109.
The show
Wayne: Up Close & Personal combines songs, stories, film clips, and audience interaction. Our performance was Monday, October 12, 2026, at 7 p.m.; future times and dates can change.
VIP experience
Ticketmaster describes a VIP Reserved option that includes a post-show meet-and-greet with Wayne, a souvenir photograph, and a commemorative laminate. Check availability and current inclusions before purchasing.
Box office & special seating
Call (702) 733-3111. Ticketmaster specifically directs requests for special seating accommodations to box-office representatives.
Showroom environment
The listed showroom is non-smoking, but it is inside a busy casino-hotel. Stage lighting, music, crowds, and the trip through the property are worth considering for sensory planning.
Age, time & parking
The Wayne Newton ticket listing specifies ages 5 and up. Allow roughly 90 minutes for the performance, more if doing VIP; confirm the current running time. Parking fees may apply.

Questions worth asking before you go

  • Arrival: Which entrance or drop-off offers the shortest confirmed step-free route to Bugsy’s Cabaret? Where are accessible parking spaces and the closest accessible restroom?
  • Seating: What wheelchair spaces, companion seats, transfer options, and aisle accommodations are available for the exact performance and ticket category?
  • Sensory access: What lighting and sound effects should guests expect? Is there a quieter waiting spot or a way to request help finding the showroom without extra wandering?
  • VIP logistics: Where does the meet-and-greet line begin, how long might the wait be, can a guest sit while waiting, and what are the mobility and companion arrangements for the photograph?
  • Other access needs: Ask directly about assisted listening, service-animal arrangements, restroom access, and any exit or re-entry policy. These features have not all been independently confirmed for this specific show.

The most useful step is to call the Flamingo box office before buying, explain the specific access needs, and ask them to confirm both the seat and the post-show VIP arrangements. A label that says “accessible” is not nearly as helpful as knowing precisely how the evening will work.
