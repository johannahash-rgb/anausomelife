Drumlin Farm Wildlife Sanctuary

Lincoln, Massachusetts

The gift of Drumlin is that it can be a real outing without becoming a project. Animals, trails, familiar geography, and home is still close.

The simple good thing

Farmyard animals, one favorite wildlife stop, snack/picnic, home.

When a family needs a reliable local option, novelty is overrated. Drumlin lets you repeat the parts that work and skip the parts that do not, which is exactly what a favorite place should allow.

Accessibility field guide

What we could verify — and what to ask.

“Accessible” is too vague to plan a real day. These are the concrete details we found. Individual needs differ, and venues change.

Verified / published

  • Mass Audubon lists accessible parking, restrooms and universally accessible All Persons Trails
  • Adaptive items are available for loan, including hands-free binoculars, audio players, a cane with a small seat and a push wheelchair
  • Mass Audubon publishes a step-by-step photo tour / Social Story for preparing a visit
  • Current sanctuary hours are generally 9–5 Tuesday–Sunday plus Monday holidays
  • Admission and program details are maintained on the official page

Ask before relying on it

  • School-group timing if crowds matter
  • Current accessible route to the specific animal/activity you want
  • Any temporary farmyard closure

A useful access question

“We’re planning to visit on [date/time]. We need [specific feature/support]. Could you tell us the most direct way to access it, where the closest restroom/break space is, and whether anything scheduled that day will substantially change crowds or the normal route?”
