How relationships are labeled

An AUsome Life may receive products, services, experiences or travel at no charge; may participate in affiliate programs; and may accept paid sponsorships. When that happens, we disclose the relationship in the relevant content.

Affiliate links may earn us a commission if you purchase through them, at no additional cost to you. Gifted product and paid work do not guarantee a positive review or inclusion in a product guide.

We do not accept payment in exchange for unsupported health claims, guaranteed positive coverage or hiding material limitations that would matter to readers.

Illustrations & photographs

The catalog-style watercolors in this edition are AI-generated editorial illustrations. They represent general ideas and objects, not photographs of a venue, proof of product ownership or an endorsement.

Family photographs already published on the site are preserved as photographs, with the people unchanged. A visual visit plan works best with current pictures of the actual places and steps your family will use.
