Disclosure.

Clear about what we made, what we know and how a recommendation is supported.

Recommendations & relationships

An AUsome Life may receive payment, products, services, experiences or travel, and may participate in affiliate programs. A material relationship belongs beside the relevant recommendation, where a reader can see it before acting—not only on this page.

Our labels say what happened: “Paid partnership with [brand],” “[Brand] provided this stay at no charge,” or “We may earn a commission if you buy through this link.” A general thank-you or the words “affiliate link” alone are not our disclosure standard. We do not add a sponsorship label when no such relationship exists.

Gifts and paid work do not guarantee a positive review. We do not invent purchases, family visits, results or endorsements. A researched suggestion, an imagined visit and something our family has tried are different kinds of content, and we identify them accordingly.

Our approach follows the FTC’s endorsement disclosure guidance.

Pictures, stories & music

Some editorial images are AI-created, including watercolor-style scenes and photorealistic picture-card and outing artwork. A realistic-looking generated image is still artwork. It is not a photograph of a family visit, a current entrance, an accessibility feature, a product we own or a condition you can rely on finding.

Our captions and collection notes identify generated imagery. Actual family photographs remain separate. A visual visit plan can use current, authorized photos of the place and steps that matter to the person.

Our publishing standard is to use our own material or material with a documented basis for reuse, including any required credit and restrictions. Finding a picture through a search engine is not permission to republish it. AI generation does not by itself establish that an output is legally cleared or that we hold exclusive copyright in it.

Original entertainment stories are labeled as fiction. Music and video need their own rights review, including the composition, recording, footage and any voice or likeness. An image license does not clear a soundtrack.

Names & independent editorial coverage

Business, venue, product and organization names identify the subjects we discuss. Their names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Our directory uses An AUsome Life editorial cards; these are not official brand logos.

Being mentioned here does not mean a business or organization sponsors, approves or endorses this site. Any actual commercial relationship must be disclosed with the relevant content. Our original characters, stories and website design are independent of the businesses and entertainment brands we discuss.

Our picture-card and visit tools are independent planning aids. They are not a branded clinical protocol, a replacement AAC system, or an official product of any AAC provider.

Practical information, with its limits

We share general information and family experience, not individual medical, legal or professional advice. Our visual tools supplement the person’s familiar communication and support; they do not promise a clinical outcome.

Venue details, prices, hours, routes and access arrangements can change. We date research, link to sources and label unconfirmed details. Check the current information with the venue before relying on it. A disclaimer does not replace careful research.

A correction or rights concern?

Please contact us with the page URL, the material concerned and a brief explanation. For a rights concern, include how you are connected to the material and any relevant source or permission information. We will review it and correct, replace or remove material when appropriate.

Please do not send private care records, identifying details about children or identity documents in an initial message.
