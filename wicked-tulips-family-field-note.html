Flowers · family photo essay

A family day in full color.

There are the photographs, and then there is the day around them. Both are worth making room for.

Wicked Tulips · a spring memory

Read the story ↓The practical details ↓

Our Wicked Tulips photographs make a persuasive argument for spring. There is a family portrait surrounded by rows of color, and another moment with tulips held in hand. The background has put in considerable effort. The people do not need to compete with it.

The family portrait and the picture with tulips in hand are the sort of photographs I am glad we kept.

Flowers have a short season. The pictures get to stay around rather longer.

Give the outing more room than the photograph.

A flower field is an inviting place to take a picture. It is also a place to look, choose, walk a little and be together. A person should be able to enjoy those things without having to hold a pose whenever the light looks promising.

Keep the photograph brief when people want one. Let choosing a color or holding a flower be a pleasure in itself. Communication belongs in the scene, even when the device or picture card is not part of the photographer’s preferred composition.

Spring does not follow our calendar.

Wicked Tulips directs visitors to its bloom reports and ticket information. Check the farm and date on the booking; a familiar name does not mean every location has the same arrangements. The flowers set the timetable, with very little interest in our other commitments.

The farm’s FAQ describes gravel approaches and ground between beds that can be soft, rocky or muddy. It also describes designated accessible parking, with field conditions still affected by rain. Ask about the actual route and conditions you need rather than treating accessible as a complete description.

Bring home flowers and a day you recognize.

The useful preparation is fairly ordinary: practical shoes, suitable layers, the usual communication and support supplies, and a clear idea of how to finish. Check the current picking instructions and ticket inclusions before deciding how much to gather.

The photographs are lovely to have afterward. So is the knowledge that the outing belonged to the people in them. Let the field supply the spectacle and give the family permission to be itself.
