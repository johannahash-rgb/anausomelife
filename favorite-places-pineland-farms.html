Our Pineland plan would begin by choosing what sounds best today, with the rest saved for another visit. A farm, a garden and a Market lunch are three possible days in one place.

Looking for practical details?

One campus, several choices

Pick the destination before the drive.

Pineland works best when the starting point is clear. Farm Education is at 110 Valley Farm Road; the Welcome Center is at 15 Farm View Drive. The official directions help you reach the right one.

If the animals are the anchor, use the farm-visit reservation page before leaving. The published visit information describes a two-hour visit window, with farm visits offered daily from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.

The Market and deli at the Welcome Center give a farm day a food option on the same campus. Check the current visit information for hours and the part you intend to use.

The local details

Before you arrive.

1Smokehouse farm check-in110 Valley Farm Road+

For the reserved farm visit, continue past the Market on Farm View Drive, take the first left onto Valley Farm Road and follow signs to the Smokehouse beside the big white silo. The visitor guide marks parking near the Dairy Barn just above it. Official farm-arrival directions · Current linked visitor guide, map pages 6–7

Check in here before the animal visit. Ask staff which currently available space and route best fit your mobility needs.

2Family FarmyardEducation Barn and nearby parking+

The guide marks parking and restrooms beside the Family Farmyard. The Education Barn houses year-round animals; your reserved farm pass covers the Farmyard and Valley Farm self-guided visit. Visitor guide and facility symbols · Farm visit and animal information

The published restroom and parking symbols do not specify wheelchair dimensions, stall types or an accessible circuit.

3Market & Welcome CenterFood, maps and a separate arrival+

The Market is at 15 Farm View Drive. The current visitor guide marks parking and restrooms here. It offers food, indoor/outdoor seating and general visitor information. Market address and hours · Current visitor guide

Normal hours are 7:30–6 weekdays and 8–6 weekends. Farm check-in is farther along Valley Farm Road.

4Perennial & Lilac GardensA smaller, separate garden stop+

For the gardens use 1 Freeport Road. The official guide marks parking at the garden approaches and picnic areas nearby; the visit page lists seasonal opening from 8 a.m. to dusk. Garden address and seasonal hours · Garden parking on current visitor guide

Do not assume the garden paths have the same surface or slope as campus roads. Confirm the particular route if mobility access determines the visit.

Sources checked October 4, 2026. Farm visits require a reserved pass and Smokehouse check-in. No universal access rating is inferred from ordinary parking/restroom map symbols.

The details that make the visit possible

Know before you go.

01Farm visits & reservations+

Use the farm-visit reservation page for the current pass and slot. The published visit information describes daily 9 a.m.–4 p.m. visits with a reservation and a two-hour window. Activity rules and seasonal availability can differ.

02Market & food+

The published visit information lists Market/Welcome Center hours of 7:30 a.m.–6 p.m. weekdays and 8 a.m.–6 p.m. weekends. Confirm the current visitor schedule and the food choices you need before relying on them.

03Addresses & parking+

The official contact and directions page separates Farm Education at 110 Valley Farm Road from the Welcome Center at 15 Farm View Drive. Use the area-specific parking and map notes above instead of assuming every activity starts together.

04Facilities & seasonal events+

Ask Pineland about the route and closest suitable bathroom for the activity you booked. The visit page has seasonal events; a corn maze or busy event can change the day’s traffic and activity level.

05A checklist to take along+
  • Choose one Pineland activity
  • Reserve if doing the farm visit
  • Save the correct address for that activity
  • Check weather
  • Locate restroom first
  • Use Market as food backup
  • Keep visit to planned window
  • Skip extra campus stops if transitions are adding up
