Autumn in New England · From the family album

Autumn, at Ruby’s pace.

James, a very busy yellow Lab, and the pleasure of an ordinary autumn visit with Mom-Mom and Grandpa Steve.

October 14, 2026 · 2 minute read

A little walk together ↓

James has the leash and a wonderful grin. Ruby has her nose in the grass and apparently quite a lot to investigate. There are leaves scattered along the road, yellow beginning to show in the trees, and still enough warmth for shorts. A very New England sort of autumn.

I love this photograph. James is looking up at the camera while Ruby gets on with the important business of being a dog. The leash makes a long white line between them. Around them, the season is quietly changing.

Time with Mom-Mom & Grandpa Steve (aka Grandma & Grandpa) is part of what makes these ordinary days so dear. A visit, a little time outside, Ruby to walk, James right in the middle of it all. Being together can be as simple as that.

Autumn in New England gives us so much to look at on an everyday walk. Green leaves hang on beside the gold ones. A few have already landed at the edge of the pavement. I like this part of the season, when summer is still showing up in somebody’s outfit and the trees have begun moving on.

And then there is Ruby, who seems perfectly prepared to spend the afternoon examining one small patch of grass. She makes a good case for taking our time. There may be something very interesting by the next leaf.

These are the family photographs I want to keep coming back to: James smiling, Ruby nearby, the people we love woven into the day. Mom-Mom and Grandpa Steve are part of that familiar, lovely world. A little walk together belongs in the family album just as much as a holiday or a birthday.

A dog, some leaves, and time with the grandparents. The simple joys of autumn, right here.
