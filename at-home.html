The house is not a showroom.

It is where everybody lands. It is where snacks happen, school papers multiply, chargers disappear and the table can hold flowers, fidgets and clutter at exactly the same time.

This section is for the small systems that genuinely earn their keep: a household instruction card, a better place for the school bag, familiar-food planning, a product we repurchase, a lower-waste swap that does not make the sink look terrible, or the very glamorous realization that sometimes butter is infrastructure.

Beauty counts. Ease counts. So does the fact that people actually live here.
