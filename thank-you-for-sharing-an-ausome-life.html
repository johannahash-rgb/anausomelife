From our family · October 15, 2026

Thank you for
passing it along.

A note to the people helping An AUsome Life reach another family.

We put a lot of care into the details before a day out: where to go, what to check, what might help, and what still needs a phone call. A useful page only does its job when someone finds it. So we want to thank the people helping that happen.

Thank you, Maine Parent Federation.

Seeing Maine Parent Federation share our Accessible New England calendar and Caroline’s Cart New England directory on Facebook meant a great deal to us.

Those links are meant for ordinary plans: a Saturday outing, a grocery trip, somewhere new to try. Thank you for putting them in front of families who may be looking for exactly that sort of information, and for the work you do alongside Maine families.

Follow Maine Parent Federation on Facebook ↗

Thank you, Domain Arrivals.

Domain Arrivals included An AUsome Life in its October 3 collection, noticing the New England outings and the practical details that go with them. We appreciate having another route for readers to find the site.

There is room here for a beautiful afternoon and the planning that makes it possible. Thank you for pointing people our way.

Read the An AUsome Life listing ↗

And to everyone who has sent a link to a friend, a parent group, or someone making a plan: thank you. We hope something here makes the next outing a little easier to arrange.

With appreciation,
An AUsome Life
