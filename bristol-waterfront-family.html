An afternoon by the harbor

A little Bristol blue.

Sailboats, stripes, and a much smaller James. A photograph from the family album, with a short guide for an afternoon on the waterfront.

Looking through old photos and found this one. Apparently, we all got the memo about wearing blue.

From the albumThe waterfront guideStaying together safely

Glad we took this one.

Bristol, Rhode Island: sailboats, sparkling water, and a family afternoon by the harbor. There is James in front, with the boats behind us and the sun on the water.

There is a practical detail in the photograph, too. James and I were each holding a voluntary walking handle as we practiced staying together safely. Nothing was attached to him; there was no tether or restraint, and he could let go at any time.

For our family, it was one way to practice while getting out and enjoying the community. Every child and family has different needs, and what worked for us may not feel right or be appropriate for someone else. It was one small part of our experience, alongside close supervision.

Mostly, I am glad we have this picture. An afternoon by the water with my two favorite people.

A small plan for a day out

Along the waterfront.

These are researched ideas for a visit today, rather than a list of places we visited on the afternoon in our photograph.

Start at Independence Park.

At 419–459 Thames Street, this four-acre park overlooks Bristol Harbor and marks the southern end of the East Bay Bike Path. The state tourism listing includes walkways and public restrooms; check restroom availability for your visit. A good starting point for watching the boats without committing to a long walk.

Independence Park visitor information

Take a waterfront wander.

The town points visitors to the boardwalk behind Stone Harbour condominiums, leading toward Rockwell Park and the Maritime Center. Choose a short stretch, stop for the view, and turn back whenever you like.

Bristol’s official places guide

Try a little of the bike path.

The East Bay Bike Path runs 13.8 miles between Providence and Bristol. You do not have to tackle the whole thing: a short out-and-back from Independence Park can be enough. Cyclists share the path, so plan the support and space your family needs.

East Bay Bike Path details

Make Audubon a separate stop.

The Audubon Nature Center and Aquarium at 1401 Hope Street has exhibits, walking trails and a boardwalk toward Narragansett Bay. Audubon describes its facility and trails as accessible. Check opening hours, admission and closure announcements before heading over.

Audubon visitor information

Before you go: check parking signs, restroom access and waterfront conditions. Bristol announced funding for seawall work at Independence Park and the Maritime Center in October 2026; the notice does not establish a closure schedule, so check local updates before visiting. Waterfront routes can include open edges, docks and boat activity.

About the walking handle & practicing staying together safely

We each held part of the handle, with James choosing to hold his. It gave us a way to practice walking together. Unexpected separation can become dangerous quickly, even with attentive caregivers.

A similar style is called Hold-On Handles. This link shows the type of product, not a confirmed match for ours; current stock and suitability need checking.

In a 2012 parent survey involving 1,218 autistic children, 49% were reported to have attempted elopement at least once after age four. That describes the surveyed group, not any individual child’s future. Read the original study in Pediatrics.

The practical, hopeful part is making room for learning and finding supports that fit. The American Academy of Pediatrics recommends teaching safety skills alongside consistent adult supervision and an individual safety plan. Read the AAP’s guidance for families.

A voluntarily held handle can be released at any moment. It cannot prevent unexpected separation or replace close supervision, particularly near water or traffic. We are sharing our experience, not general advice or a recommendation for restraint. Neither source establishes that a walking handle reduces elopement.

Different people need different supports. For us, the point was getting out together and having opportunities to practice.
